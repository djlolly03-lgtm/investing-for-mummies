# What to say — 19 Aug

**Hiral · Ketaki · Asba · Sakshi · 45 min.** Everything in quotes is yours to say. Everything else is a stage direction.

---

### 1 · OPEN *(5 min)* — don't start on the org chart

> **"We've all been working hard on the same thing without ever writing down what the thing is. That's my gap, not yours."**
>
> **"So today I want to show you the whole picture — where we are, what the numbers say, and where we're going. And then you'll see why the one empty box is empty."**
>
> **"Nobody in this room dropped the ball."**

### 2 · THE EVIDENCE *(8 min)* — say the numbers flat, no names attached

> **"358 followers. Growing about one and a half a day."**
>
> **"Here's the surprising bit — the people who do see us engage *more* than most of our competitors' audiences do. The content isn't the problem."**
>
> **"Zero of our last twelve posts asked the reader to do anything."**
>
> **"Twenty-seven people raised their hand and nobody followed up."**
>
> **"Ninety-nine of our hundred and sixteen students have never been asked for feedback."**
>
> **"So — we're not short of interest. We're short of anyone whose job it is to catch it."**

### 3 · THE MODEL *(7 min)* — org chart on screen. Slow down here.

> **"Three lanes. The boundary between them is the payment."**
>
> **"Two of them are staffed. The middle one never existed — it got smeared across people who didn't want it and didn't take upside from it. So of course it didn't get done."**

### 4 · WHAT IT MEANS *(7 min)* — role level only. No targets, no dates.

> **"Sakshi — Broadcast. She brings people to the door."**
>
> **"Asba — everything from the moment they pay. And Asba, the two-leads-a-month target is withdrawn. That was never your lane."**
>
> **"The middle box — that's the hire."**
>
> **"Hiral — teaching, curriculum, the brand, and her own story, which we've barely told."**

### 5 · THE HIRE *(8 min)*

> **"Enrolment and Growth Executive. Twenty-five to forty thousand fixed, no incentive until we've enrolled twenty students — because until then any number we set is a guess."**
>
> **"Break-even is about two seats a month. A full cycle is thirty-six to forty."**
>
> **"And we don't interview them. We give them a live mock call — because that's the job."**

### 6 · THE MONEY ALREADY ON THE TABLE *(5 min)* — close the presenting here

> **"Seventeen September seats are unsold. That's about three point two lakh, in capacity we've already built and paid for."**
>
> **"A hundred and forty-three warm contacts. It doesn't need Instagram, or the agency, or anything we're building. It needs someone to make the calls."**

### 7 · ALIGNMENT ROUND *(10 min)* — **stop presenting. This is the part that matters.**

> **"Before we finish — I want to hear it back. In one sentence each: what do you own?"**
>
> *Round the room. Sakshi, Asba, Ketaki, then you.*
>
> **"Where does this break? You know the handoffs better than I do."**
>
> **"And genuinely — what worries you about this?"**

⚠️ **Don't rescue a fuzzy answer by re-explaining.** Ask them to try again in their own words.
⚠️ **If nobody disagrees with anything, you broadcast — you didn't align.** Push once.

### 8 · CLOSE

> **"All of this lives on the Momentum Board from Monday. The documents explain the why. The board is where we'll see whether it's actually happening."**
