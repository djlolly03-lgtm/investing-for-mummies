# Team Roadmap — the next 12 months

**Prepared:** 18 August 2026
**Companion to:** `1 - IFM Role Architecture`

---

## Start with the constraint, not the org chart

The instinct when a business isn't converting is to hire more marketing and sales. IFM is doing that, correctly, right now.

**But it's worth naming what breaks next — because it isn't sales.**

> ### Hiral teaches every single batch.

Two batches a month, eight sessions each, two hours a session, **18–20 students per room once the rebuild is done.** That's **32 teaching hours a month before any preparation** — and she also owns curriculum, the alumni relationships, the founder brand, institutional pitching, and a book.

**Every hire below is sequenced around that one fact.**

---

## The ceiling, in numbers

| | |
|---|---|
| Batches per month | 2 *(Mon/Wed + Tue/Thu)* |
| Seats per batch | **15–18 today · 18–20 once the new room is built** |
| Seats per cycle | **36–40** once the new room is ready — 18–20 per batch |
| Current list price | ₹19,000–21,000 |
| **Monthly revenue ceiling** | **₹6.8L–₹7.6L** once the room is ready |
| **Annual ceiling at full capacity** | **₹82L–₹91L** |

**That ceiling is not close yet** — 72 mums have enrolled in the business's entire lifetime, so filling 36–40 seats every month would be roughly six times that, in one year.

**Which is the point.** Sales is the right hire now because there is a large gap between current sales and existing capacity. **The teaching constraint only bites once that gap closes** — and the roadmap should be triggered by that happening, not by a calendar.

---

## Hire against triggers, not dates

Every role below has a number attached. **If the number hasn't happened, don't hire.** Hiring ahead of a constraint is how small companies run out of money while looking busy.

### 🟢 NOW — the room rebuild *(in progress, not a hire)*

A dedicated IFM room is being built in the office, sized for **18–20 students**. It runs in parallel with the sales hire and neither waits for the other.

**Why it matters here:** it raises the ceiling from ~15–18 a batch to 18–20 — roughly **36–40 mothers a month across both batches** — and it does so before the sales hire is at full productivity. **The capacity arrives just as the demand does.**

---

### 🟢 SEPTEMBER — Meta ads, ₹20–30k/month *(spend, not a hire)*

**Goal: sign-ups on the batch page. Not followers, not reach.** At the ₹19k early-bird price, break-even on ₹30k is ~1.5 seats; a modest 4–6 seats/month is a 3–4× return.

**Homework before a rupee is spent:**
- **Meta Pixel on the registration page** — count sign-ups, not clicks
- **A persuasion layer above the form** — ads land cold, and today the page is a checkout
- **Creative from what already works** — faces: workshop footage, certificate photos (consent done first)
- **Geo-target Mumbai** — classes are in person at Nariman Point
- **A META coupon code** — every ad seat traceable, same pattern as ASBA001
- **A named owner** — agency scope or freelancer, decided before September

> Ads amplify whatever they land on. Pointed at a page that can convert and a pixel that can count, ₹30k is 4–6 seats. Pointed at today's setup, it's just reach.

### 🟡 NEXT — Co-teacher
**This is the most important hire in this document, and it is not a marketing hire.**

**Trigger: both batches full — 36–40 mothers a month — for two consecutive cycles.** That is the agreed sequence: fill the room first, then add teaching capacity. Not a date.

**Why it matters more than anything else here:** until someone other than Hiral can teach, revenue is capped at whatever one person can deliver in a month. **This is the only hire that raises the ceiling rather than filling the room.**

**What must exist first:**
- The curriculum documented well enough to be taught by someone else — **8 sessions, session by session**
- Asba's SOP, so the operational wrapper is repeatable
- A settled view on the insurance module and anything else flagged as weak

**Where to find them:** most likely a past student. Someone who has been through the programme, believes in it, and can present. **A co-teacher, not a replacement** — they teach alongside Hiral before teaching alone. Prior teaching experience matters far less than credibility with the audience and willingness to be trained into the curriculum.

**Route:** shadow two full batches → co-teach one → teach one with Hiral observing.

> **This hire is also the franchise test.** If a second person can deliver the same programme at the same quality, Guwahati becomes possible. If they can't, the business is a consultancy with Hiral's name on it — which is a fine business, just a different one, and worth choosing deliberately.

### 🟡 NEXT — Advanced course *(product, not a hire)*

**Trigger:** whenever Hiral has bandwidth — most realistically **after the co-teacher takes some teaching load.**

72 alumni currently have nothing to buy. That is roughly **₹1.4L of forgone revenue** at current pricing, from people who have already paid once and enjoyed it — the cheapest revenue available anywhere in the business.

**Note the dependency:** it needs Hiral's design time, and she won't have it until teaching load comes down. **The co-teacher unlocks the advanced course, which then justifies more sales capacity.** That's the growth loop.

| **Institutional pitching** | B2C runs predictably | A dedicated institutional seller, or stays and is accepted as hers |
| **Alumni relationships** | **Probably never — and that's correct.** They came through her personally. | — |
| **Curriculum design** | Never. It's the product. | — |

**The honest read:** Hiral cannot hand over teaching *and* curriculum *and* the book *and* the brand simultaneously. **The co-teacher is the one that buys time for the rest**, which is why it ranks above every other future hire here.

## The sequence in one line — as agreed 18 August

> **Hire sales while the room is built → sales fills both batches to 36–40 mothers → then hire a co-teacher → then build the advanced course.**

**The first two run in parallel**, which is what makes this fast: the room is being rebuilt now, and by the time it's ready the sales hire should be producing.

Not before. The trigger for the trainer is both batches full, not a date.

Each step funds and enables the next. **The failure mode is hiring sales capacity beyond teaching capacity** — selling seats into batches that don't exist, which converts a growth problem into a refund problem.
