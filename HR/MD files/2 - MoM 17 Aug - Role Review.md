## 3. SOP — the main piece of work for Asba

**Assessment given directly:** *"In the last 2–3 months you have stagnated — you haven't pushed yourself to figure out what to do next."* The SOP is what to do next, and it is already on the Momentum Board.

### Why now
Several batches have run. The conditions for a real SOP exist:
- **Batch dates are hard-coded** — 1 September means 1 September, never moved
- **The curriculum is now identical batch to batch**

Both facts make the process highly automatable.

### How to build it
1. Pen and paper, or Whisper Flow.
2. Start at the moment of payment. Auto-email fires. **Then map every single interaction with that person until they finish the programme.**
3. Include the variables, not just the happy path — someone joins late, someone wants one session online, someone moves Monday to Tuesday.
4. **Iterate: list 1 → sleep on it → list 2 → list 3 → list 4 or 5**, until it covers ~99.9% of everything ever done with any student.
5. That becomes the first-pass SOP.
6. Hand it to Claude to identify what can be automated. **N8N is the automation layer — Suraj and Aniket have access.**

> *"'I will handle all of this' is not an answer. It has to be drilled down into exactly what you are doing."*

### Why it matters beyond convenience
**Franchise readiness.** If IFM goes the franchise route and finds a "hero number 2" in Guwahati doing exactly this, she needs the SOP daily. *"There's a science behind how McDonald's got there."*

**Certification is named as an example** — at the end of every batch a certificate is issued, and that has to flow onto the content hub. Every small recurring thing like this belongs in the SOP.

## 4. Feedback — restructure into two layers

### The problem
- The current form is long and option-based ("which topic did you like"). **It produces no written testimonials** — nothing usable as social proof.
- More seriously: **the curriculum is set by what Hiral believes is right, and nobody is asking the students.** Nobody has asked "should we spend 20% more time on stocks and less on insurance, more on REITs, none on gold?"
- Live example: Hiral reported Monday and Tuesday batches were **"very dry" on the insurance topic.** That signal should trigger a rework of the insurance module — and currently there is no mechanism that would surface it systematically.

### The decision
**Split feedback into two instruments:**

| | When | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Session feedback** | End of **every** session | Per-topic signal — *"gold worked, insurance didn't"*. Drives curriculum improvement. |
| **Programme feedback** | End of the full programme | Overall experience, and **written testimonials** |

Simplify both. The current form asks too much and captures too little. **To be discussed with Hiral.**

This sits inside the retention SOP — feedback collection is a scheduled step, not an afterthought.
