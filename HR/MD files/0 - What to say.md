# What to say — slide by slide

**25 slides · 45 min.** Roughly **25 min presenting, 10 min alignment round, 10 min spare.**
Everything in quotes is yours to say. Italics are stage directions.

> ⚠️ **Slide 19 says 356 followers, slide 2 says 358.** If anyone spots it: *"358 as of this morning — the deck was built yesterday."* It's a good problem.

---

## OPEN · slides 1–2 *(4 min)*

**Slide 1 — Where we go next**

> **"We've all been working hard on the same thing without ever writing down what the thing is. That's my gap, not yours."**
>
> **"So today — the whole picture. Where we are, what the numbers say, where we're going. And then you'll see why one box is empty."**
>
> **"Nobody in this room dropped the ball."**

**Slide 2 — A business that teaches well**

> **"116 students. ₹15.65 lakh. 358 followers — 344 a week ago, so it's moving."**
>
> **"The people who find us, stay. Retention and teaching are not the problem, and this deck isn't about fixing them."**

---

## THE EVIDENCE · slides 3–7 *(7 min — move briskly, don't dwell)*

**Slide 3 — 4.7 vs 3.3**
> **"Comments per hundred likes. We're at 4.7, the competitor median is 3.3. The people who see us engage more than most of our competitors' audiences do."**
>
> **"So the content isn't the problem. There just aren't enough people seeing it."**

**Slide 4 — Real rooms beat explainers**
> **"Three carefully produced finance explainers: zero comments between them. One photo of a real workshop: ten."**
>
> **"Our most effortful content performs worst."**

**Slide 5 — It's faces**
> **"Sixteen of the seventeen comments this account has ever received landed on a post with a face in it."**
>
> *(point at the yellow strip)* **"And here's the honest wrinkle — our worst reel ever was the same subject as our winners. Backs of heads, under a text overlay, framed as a promo. Faces plus a story beats faceless plus a promo, on the exact same subject."**

**Slide 6 — Certificate photo**
> **"So the repeatable format is obvious: every batch ends in a certificate photo. And the buyer isn't the teen — it's the mum who sees it."**
>
> **"One caveat, and it's not optional: these are minors with their names on the certificates. Parental consent first, names illegible."**

**Slide 7 — Position uncontested**
> **"Zero of thirteen tracked competitors target mums. Zero have a single interactive game. We have eleven."**

---

## THE GAPS · slide 8 *(3 min)* — say these flat, attach no names

> **"Zero of our last twelve posts asked the reader to do anything."**
>
> **"Twenty-seven people raised their hand. Nobody followed up."**
>
> **"Ninety-nine of our hundred and sixteen students have never been asked for feedback."**
>
> **"And not one rupee of revenue is traceable to a source."**
>
> **"Every one of those is a follow-up failure, not a content failure. And they share a single cause."**

---

## WHY WE ARE NOT BEHIND · slide 9 *(3 min)* — **slow down, this is the reframe**

> **"Every creator we track built an audience first, then invented a course to sell to it. We've done it the other way round — 116 students, batches running, curriculum proven — and now we're building the audience."**
>
> **"They had to manufacture proof. We already have it."**
>
> **"We're not behind on the business. We're behind on being findable, and that's a much smaller problem."**

---

## THE INSIGHT · slide 10 *(2 min)* — **pause. Let it sit.**

> **"We are not short of interest. We are short of anyone whose job it is to catch it."**
>
> **"Sales was never a role here. It got smeared across people who didn't want it and didn't take upside from it — so of course it didn't get done."**

---

## THE MODEL · slides 11–12 *(5 min)* — **the most important minutes**

**Slide 11 — Three lanes**
> **"Three lanes. The boundary between them is the payment."**
>
> **"Two are staffed. The middle one never existed."**
>
> **"An enquiry hands over the same day. Payment clears, and it becomes Asba's."**

**Slide 12 — Roles evolving**
> **"Sakshi — Broadcast. She brings people to the door. New for her: fifteen outbound comments a day."**
>
> **"Asba — everything from the moment they pay. And Asba, the two-leads-a-month target is withdrawn. That was never your lane."**
>
> **"Hiral — teaching, curriculum, brand, alumni. Handing over the follow-ups."**
>
> **"Nobody loses scope. Everybody gets a boundary."**

---

## THE PLAYBOOK · slides 13–20 *(6 min total — this section is for everyone to learn from, but keep it moving)*

**Slide 13 — Sakshi's day**
> **"Three hours, four blocks. The seventy-five minutes of outbound is the growth engine — it never gets skipped."**
>
> **"Two standing musts: film every class, and the first hour of every post she walks the office and gets real conversations going."**

**Slides 14–16 — the three live examples** *(let them read; talk over the pattern, not the detail)*
> **"Three real posts from this week, three different moves. React with the mum's reality. Add the honest footnote. Respond to her, not the topic."**
>
> **"The pattern is at the bottom: read it first, say something only IFM could say, take a small position, never pitch."**

**Slide 17 — Replies**
> **"Every reply has to invite the next message. And when someone asks the price — price, date, next step. Dodging loses her, and so does pouncing."**

**Slide 18 — Twelve captions, zero asks**
> **"Zero calls to action. Zero prices. Zero dates."**
>
> **"Sakshi reached exactly the same conclusion on her own, studying competitors — not a single Instagram video mentions a course."**

**Slide 19 — 356 followers and no way in**
> **"Our bio ends with a finger pointing down — at nothing. And one tab away is a page with live seat counts and a Pay button."**
>
> *(if fixed)* **"We fixed it last night."**

**Slide 20 — The fix**
> **"One line added at the end. The caption itself doesn't change."**

## THE HIRE · slide 21 *(4 min)*

> **"Enrolment and Growth Executive. Twenty-five to forty thousand fixed. No incentive until we've enrolled twenty students — because until then any number we set is a guess."**
>
> **"Break-even is about two seats a month, against a cycle of thirty-six to forty."**
>
> **"And we don't interview them. We give them a live mock call, because that's the job."**

---

## THE MONEY ON THE TABLE · slide 22 *(2 min)*

> **"Seventeen September seats unsold. About ₹3.2 lakh, in capacity we've already built and paid for."**
>
> **"The gap isn't demand. Every unsold seat costs us the same to deliver as a sold one."**

---

## THE PLAN · slides 23–25 *(3 min)*

**Slide 23 — Three moves**
> **"Hire sales while the room is being built — those run in parallel. Fill both batches to thirty-six, forty mothers. Then, and only then, a co-teacher."**
>
> **"Triggered by full batches, not by a date."**

**Slide 24 — Meta ads**
> **"Twenty to thirty thousand from September. The goal is sign-ups, not followers — and the homework happens before a single rupee is spent."**

**Slide 25 — Already in motion**
> **"None of this is a proposal. Six things are already moving."**
>
> **"One target ahead of everything else: both batches full."**

# THE ALIGNMENT ROUND *(10 min)* — **stop presenting. Screen off if you can.**

> **"Before we finish, I want to hear it back. One sentence each: what do you own?"**

*Round the room — Sakshi, Asba, Ketaki, then you.*

> **"Where does this break? You know the handoffs better than I do."**

> **"And genuinely — what worries you about this?"**

⚠️ **Don't rescue a fuzzy answer by re-explaining.** Ask them to say it again in their own words.
⚠️ **If nobody disagrees with anything, you broadcast — you didn't align.** Push once.

**Close:**
> **"All of this lives on the Momentum Board from Monday. The documents explain the why. The board is where we'll see whether it's actually happening."**

## Get before they leave

- Seats per batch — and who owns the first-Friday publish
- Approve the hire: owner + start-by date
- Confirm: individual seats only, all three programmes. Schools stay with Hiral + Asba
- Where do registration-page waitlist leads go?

## Do not say

- ✗ *"You've stagnated"*
- ✗ Anything making the 27 prospects sound like Asba's failure
- ✗ Sakshi's pay, review or targets in front of the others

## If challenged

**"Is my curriculum wrong?"**
> **"Not at all. You found the insurance session was dry because you were in the room and you noticed. That shouldn't depend on you noticing — ask after every session and the data tells you."**

**"Can we afford another hire?"**
> **"Two seats a month against a cycle of thirty-six to forty. And twenty-seven people are already waiting. It costs more not to."**

**"Why can't Sakshi do the follow-ups?"**
> **"She logs them and hands them over. Pressure-selling to women who are nervous about money costs us more than a missed seat."**
