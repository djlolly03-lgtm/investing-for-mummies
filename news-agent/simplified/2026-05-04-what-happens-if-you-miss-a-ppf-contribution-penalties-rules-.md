**Missed Your PPF Payment? Here's What To Do Next**

PPF (Public Provident Fund) is a safe, government-backed savings scheme that many Indian families use to build long-term wealth. But what if you forget to deposit money one year? Don't panic — it's fixable, and the penalty is very small.

**What this means for you:**
- **You must deposit at least ₹500 every year** to keep your PPF account active. If you miss a year, your account becomes "inactive" — meaning you can't add fresh money or take loans against it.
- **Reactivating is simple and cheap.** For each year you missed, pay just ₹50 as a fine plus the minimum ₹500 deposit. So if you missed 2 years, you pay ₹1,100 total. Then submit a form at your bank or post office — and you're back on track!
- **Try to deposit before the 5th of each month.** PPF calculates interest on your balance between the 5th and month-end. Depositing early means you earn more interest — like getting a little bonus for being organised!
- **PPF is triple tax-free (called "EEE").** Your deposit saves you tax, the interest you earn is tax-free, and the money you get back at the end is also tax-free. Very few investments offer this.

**The bottom line:**
Missing a PPF payment is not the end of the world — a small fine gets your account back on track, so don't let one missed year stop your savings journey.
