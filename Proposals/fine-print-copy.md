## Card 1 — Confirmation & payment

The programme is confirmed on receipt of 100% of the fee in advance, together with completed registration forms for all participants. A GST tax invoice with IFM's GSTIN is issued on payment — fees are eligible for input tax credit. Where TDS is deducted under Section 194J, please share Form 16A. Fees are non-transferable once the programme is confirmed.

## Card 2 — Cancellation & rescheduling

Once dates are fixed, one session may be rescheduled with at least 72 hours' notice; a make-up slot is agreed within the programme window. All other sessions run as scheduled. If IFM ever has to move a session — trainer illness or events beyond anyone's control — a make-up session is arranged at no extra cost. All eight sessions are always delivered.

## Card 3 — Group size & participation

Fees are for a private, closed group of up to 10 participants. Registration is mandatory for every participant and the list is fixed at confirmation; substitutions are welcome until Session 1. Participants who miss a session receive that session's materials.

## Card 4 — Venue & logistics

For the "at your location" format, the client arranges the room, seating, projector/screen and Wi-Fi, and is responsible for venue safety and access; IFM brings all materials and printed handbooks. For online delivery, IFM hosts the video link and handbooks are couriered to one client address before Session 1.

## Card 5 — No financial advice

IFM sessions are educational and do not constitute investment, tax or legal advice. IFM is not a SEBI-registered Investment Adviser or Research Analyst, gives no personalised recommendations, names no securities or schemes to buy or sell, and earns no commission or referral fee from any financial product or provider. Decisions participants take are their own; IFM accepts no liability for investment outcomes.

## Card 6 — Confidentiality & use of content

Handbooks and materials are IFM's intellectual property, for participants' personal use only — not for redistribution — and sessions may not be recorded by participants or the client. IFM photographs or films only with prior written consent: the registration form carries an optional opt-in, and only participants who opt in appear in IFM's marketing. Registration details are used solely to run the programme and are never shared.

## Card 7 (new) — Validity

This proposal and its pricing are valid for 14 days from the cover date. The proposed month holds for confirmations — payment and all registration forms — received within that window; later confirmations move to the next available month. The session calendar is planned around festival weeks.
