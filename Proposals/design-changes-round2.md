# Women & Wealth proposal — round 2 change list (20 Aug 2026)

Three edits to the proposal PDF. Exact text below — no other pages change. These are written to
stay consistent with the Service Agreement (see companion file) — the Agreement is the document
of record and the proposal now says so.

## 1. New line — proposal is indicative, not binding

**Page 07 · Terms & Conditions** — add directly under the "The fine print 📋" heading, above
clause 01, as a short italic line:

> *This proposal is indicative and does not itself create a binding obligation on either party.
> Final terms — payment, cancellation, liability, IP and data protection — are set out in the
> signed Service Agreement, which prevails over this proposal in the event of any conflict.*

## 2. New clause — tiered client cancellation & refund (replaces the flat "non-transferable")

Insert as a **new clause 02**, renumbering the current clauses 02–08 to 03–09.

**Clause 01 (Confirmation & payment)** — change the last sentence from
"Fees are non-transferable once confirmed."
to:
"Fees are non-transferable once confirmed, except as set out in Clause 02 below."

**New Clause 02 · Client cancellation & refunds**

> If the client cancels before Session 1, the advance is refunded on a sliding scale based on
> notice given:
>
> | Notice before Session 1 | Refund |
> |---|---|
> | 14 days or more | 75% |
> | 7–13 days | 50% |
> | 3–6 days | 25% |
> | Under 72 hours, or no-show | 0% — a Credit Note valid 6 months toward a future IFM programme is issued instead |
>
> If the client discontinues after Session 1 has begun, fees for sessions already delivered are
> non-refundable. For sessions not yet delivered, the client may either complete them within 60
> days of the original last session date, or discontinue and receive a Credit Note for 50% of
> their value, valid 6 months.
>
> If IFM cancels two or more sessions for reasons other than the single reschedule or force
> majeure covered in Clause 03, the client may terminate and receive a full refund for any
> undelivered sessions.
