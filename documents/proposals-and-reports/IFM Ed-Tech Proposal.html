From Classroom to Ed-Tech

Which platform should carry IFM online — Thinkific, Teachable, LearnWorlds, Podia, TalentLMS, LearnDash, or an Indian counterpart — and the phased plan to go from physical-only workshops to a hybrid education business.

Based on three research tracks (Aug 2026): global platforms · Indian platforms · funnel strategy, pricing benchmarks & SEBI compliance. Prices verified against official pages; re-check before purchase.

The verdict, up front

None of the six you named is the best fit. IFM has two non-negotiable requirements that most platforms fail: (1) your 11 games must embed playable inside lessons (they're iframes on Vercel), and (2) you must collect INR via UPI with GST invoices. Podia bans iframes outright; Teachable pays Indian creators by PayPal once a month, two months late; Thinkific, LearnWorlds and TalentLMS all depend on Stripe — which is effectively closed to new Indian merchants.

Recommendation: start with no LMS at all for 60 days (Zoom + Razorpay payment pages + WhatsApp — ₹0 platform cost), then adopt Graphy (₹24,999/yr Launch plan) as your course home — it's Indian, Razorpay/UPI/GST-native, has documented iframe embedding inside lessons, native live classes, and is the exact platform CA Rachana Ranade built her empire on. Fallback: LearnDash on your existing WordPress (₹25–50k/yr all-in, zero transaction fees, total ownership) if Graphy's trial disappoints or its 10% entry-tier fee stings at scale.

1 · Why the games decide everything

IFM's unfair advantage online is the same as offline: nobody else's finance class has multiplayer QR-join games. Stock Rush, KBC, LTM, Sapno Ka Ghar and the rest already live on Vercel as self-contained web apps — which means they can embed in any platform that allows an iframe inside a lesson, and they keep working even if you switch platforms later. The games are platform-independent; the LMS is replaceable. That's the right architecture, and it's already built.

So the platform question reduces to three filters: can it iframe? can it take UPI? can one person run it?

4 · Recommended stack & decision logic

Phase 0 — now (₹0/mo)

Zoom Pro (₹1,376/mo) + Razorpay payment pages + WhatsApp Business (free) + existing Vercel games & WordPress. Run the whole funnel with no LMS while you validate demand.

Phase 1 — Graphy Launch (₹24,999/yr)

Adopt once ~50+ paying students exist. Trial first: verify your game iframes render un-sanitized inside a lesson on the Launch tier — that single test is the go/no-go. Upgrade to Grow later to cut the fee to 7.5% and unlock white-label WhatsApp.

Fallback — LearnDash (₹25–50k/yr)

If Graphy fails the embed test, feels shaky (Unacademy risk), or the 10% fee bites: LearnDash Essentials ($259/yr) on your existing WordPress — Razorpay/UPI is built into LearnDash core, GST via WooCommerce. Zero fees, perfect embedding, full ownership — and unusually viable for IFM because Claude already maintains your web stack.

Hedges (always)

Games stay on Vercel (never upload to any platform). Video masters stay on your drive. Export learner emails/phone numbers monthly. The platform is a tenant, not the landlord.

5 · The funnel: free virtual class as the hook

This is the model every successful Indian finance educator runs, adapted to IFM's one true differentiator — nobody else's free webinar has live multiplayer games.

  1. Free "Money Games Night" masterclass — 90 min on Zoom: one QR-join game + one teaching block. Instagram → registration → WhatsApp group → reminders → live session. Attendees scan and play from their phones exactly like your physical classes.
  2. ₹199–499 paid mini-workshop (2 hr) — the "1% Club move": even ₹99 entry roughly doubles show-up rates and pre-qualifies buyers.
  3. Flagship live cohort — "Money Confident Mummies" ₹4,999 — 4–6 weekly Zoom sessions, 25–40 seats, games as the spine of every session, WhatsApp group between sessions, replays included. Raise to ₹6,999 after two sold-out runs.
  4. Gen Z track ₹999–1,999 + corporate virtual at ₹40–60k — student pricing sold via college gigs; corporate stays your highest-margin leg.
  5. Alumni membership — "IFM Circle" ₹499/qtr or ₹4,999 lifetime — monthly live game night + Q&A + community. The continuity revenue layer (1% Club's ₹16,999 lifetime proves Indians pay for this).

Plan with honest numbers. Registration→attendance runs 35–50%; attendee→buyer for a mid-ticket offer is realistically 3–5% for cold audiences (your warm Instagram + past-workshop audience will do better). So: 1,000 registrants → ~450 attend → ~15–25 cohort buyers. WhatsApp — not email — is the nurture channel for this demographic; graduate from the free WhatsApp Business app to AiSensy (from ₹1,500/mo) only when broadcasts outgrow manual sending.

Why live cohorts, not a video library

Self-paced courses complete at 3–15%; cohort-based at 64–90%. LXME — the closest women-focused analog, 1.5M members — built entirely on live sessions + games + community, not recorded libraries. Completion matters commercially: completers refer, and referral is the cheapest acquisition for the mummies segment. Recordings are a replay perk, never the product.

7 · Staying on the right side of SEBI

The 2024–25 finfluencer crackdown (Reg 16A + Jan/Feb 2025 circulars) is actually good news for IFM — it clears out tip-sellers while explicitly protecting pure investor education. The lines:

Allowed without any registration: teaching budgeting, SIPs, compounding, mutual-fund mechanics, insurance, tax — everything your games do. Paid literacy courses and workshops. Historical market data older than 3 months.

Never do (without RIA registration): name real stocks with recent prices, imply future returns, show live trades or live market data, run groups guiding market positions.

Hygiene: games keep using fictional companies (they already do); a standard "educational simulation, not investment advice" disclaimer on everything; no "guaranteed returns" language in marketing. Staying cleanly education-only also preserves the right to do paid partnerships with AMCs and brokers later — regulated entities may only associate with unregistered persons who are pure educators.

9 · Risks & mitigations

  • Graphy/Unacademy instability — real but hedged: games on Vercel, videos offline, monthly data exports; migration to LearnDash is days, not months.
  • Graphy's 10% entry fee — at ₹1.5L/cohort that's ₹15k/cohort; the Grow tier (₹49,999/yr, 7.5%) pays for itself around ₹10L/yr of platform sales. Track and upgrade.
  • Free-webinar no-shows — WhatsApp reminders + the ₹99–199 paid-qualifier tier are the proven fixes.
  • Founder time — the funnel is designed so one person runs it: 2 masterclasses + 1 mini-workshop per month, one cohort per quarter. Resist launching everything at once.
  • Regulatory drift — SEBI is tightening further on live-market-data "education"; IFM's simulation-based approach is on the safest ground and should stay there.
