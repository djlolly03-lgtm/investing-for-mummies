Having money and knowing how to make it work are two very different things.

You earn, save and invest. But how does inflation affect your money? Why does the RBI change interest rates? And are your investments actually working towards your goals?

A practical, interactive workshop that connects these dots through analogies, real-life examples, games and hands-on exercises.

Not about complicated products or becoming an expert. About understanding the fundamentals well enough to make informed decisions.

How do I make sense of my money?

Give every rupee a job.

Organise your money with the Protect, Stabilise & Grow framework.

STABILISEStable, accessible money for nearer-term needs
GROWLong-term wealth that beats inflation
LET’S MAKE IT PRACTICALPlay the 3 Bucket Game, then look at what each of your investments is doing for you.
Takeaway: See your portfolio as investments with different jobs.
