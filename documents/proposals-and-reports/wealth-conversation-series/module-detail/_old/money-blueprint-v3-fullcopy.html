Why should my money work?

Understand why earning and saving money is only the beginning.

Money sitting in your bank account may look unchanged, but its purchasing power doesn’t stay the same.

In this conversation, we’ll explore why money needs to grow, what affects its value and how time can become one of your biggest allies in building wealth.

From the workshop · the two forces at workREAL SCREENS FROM OUR GAMES
Inflation, liveType in what you spend today and watch its buying power shrink.
Compounding, live₹5,000 a month until 60, for every age in the room. Time does most of the work.

What we’ll explore

Saving vs InvestingUnderstand the difference between keeping money aside and putting it to work.
Inflation – The Silent Money EaterDiscover how inflation makes everyday things more expensive and how the cost of achieving your financial goals changes over time.
The RBI and Interest RatesUnderstand what the repo rate is, why the RBI changes it and how interest rate decisions are used to influence inflation. See how these decisions can affect your savings, loans and investments.
The Power of CompoundingDiscover how your money can earn returns, and how those returns can generate further returns over time.
The Power of TimeUnderstand why starting early and staying invested can make a meaningful difference to long-term wealth creation.
LET’S MAKE IT PRACTICAL
  • Use an inflation calculator to see how much more your future goals could cost.
  • Take everyday lifestyle spending and explore what investing that amount through a monthly SIP could potentially create over time.
  • Participate in live compounding exercises to see how money grows and how returns build upon returns.

How should I think about my money?

Move from investing randomly to investing with purpose.

Before choosing an investment, you need to know what you’re investing for.

A holiday, a child’s education, buying a home and retirement are very different goals. They have different timelines, different financial requirements and different levels of risk you may be comfortable taking.

In this conversation, we’ll introduce a simple framework to help you think through your financial goals before deciding where to invest.

From the workshop · turning a wish into a goalREAL SCREENS FROM OUR GAMES
Goals by timelineShort, medium and long term, each with its target and how much is already funded.
From a wish to a numberA child’s education, 15 years away: today’s cost, inflation and the monthly SIP it needs.

What we’ll explore

Defining Financial GoalsMove beyond vague aspirations and identify what you want your money to achieve.
The SMART FrameworkLearn how to turn broad financial wishes into Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant and Time-bound goals.
Time HorizonUnderstand why the time available to achieve a goal influences how you should approach your investments.
Risk and ReturnExplore the relationship between the risk you take and the returns you hope to earn.
Matching Investments to GoalsUnderstand why the right investment depends on what you’re trying to achieve, when you need the money and how much risk you can take.
LET’S MAKE IT PRACTICAL
  • Work through examples and exercises to turn everyday financial aspirations into clearly defined, measurable goals.
Your
takeaway
A simple framework to evaluate financial decisions, set meaningful goals and think about investments in the context of your life.

How do I make sense of my money?

Give every rupee a job.

Once you know what you’re saving and investing for, the next question is: How do you organise your money?

Should everything be invested for growth? How much should remain accessible? What should protect you against unexpected events? And how do you bring all these investments together?

In this conversation, we’ll introduce the Protect, Stabilise & Grow framework — a simple way to understand the different jobs your money needs to do.

The 3 Bucket GameEach product appears on screen; the room decides which bucket it belongs in.
What we’ll explore · the three jobs your money does
PROTECTUnderstand the importance of financial protection through emergency funds, health insurance and term insurance.
STABILISEExplore the role of relatively stable and accessible investments in providing financial security and meeting nearer-term needs.
GROWUnderstand how growth-oriented investments can help build long-term wealth and combat inflation.
Your goals→Protect · Stabilise · Grow→Investment products

We’ll then connect these three buckets to financial goals and explore how different investment products can serve different purposes.

The idea is to stop looking at investments as isolated products and start seeing them as parts of a larger financial picture.

LET’S MAKE IT PRACTICAL
  • Play the 3 Bucket Game! Through an interactive exercise, you’ll learn how to categorise different investments, allocate money to different needs and think about building a more balanced portfolio.
  • We’ll also explore how to look at your existing portfolio and understand what each investment is actually doing for you.
Your
takeaway
A simple framework to organise your investments, understand asset allocation and look at your portfolio as a collection of investments with different jobs.

How do I join the conversation?

Because understanding your money also means knowing what questions to ask.

You don’t need to be a financial expert to participate in decisions about your wealth.

But you do need to know what to ask, what to look for and when to seek clarification.

In this final conversation, we’ll bring everything together and explore how to become a more informed participant in conversations about your finances.

What we’ll explore

Understanding Financial LanguageGet comfortable with commonly used financial terms and investment terminology.
Understanding What You OwnLearn to look at your investments in the context of your goals and the role they play in your portfolio.
Talking to Your Financial AdvisorUnderstand how to have meaningful conversations about your investments rather than simply accepting recommendations.
Asking the Right QuestionsLearn what to ask about risk, returns, costs, liquidity, time horizons and suitability.
Participating in Financial DecisionsUnderstand how to take a more active role in conversations and decisions that affect your financial future.

The bigger picture

You don’t have to become an expert in the stock market to understand your money. You do, however, need to understand what your money is doing, why it is invested the way it is, and whether it is helping you get closer to the life you want.

The Money Blueprint is where that conversation begins.

Because financial awareness isn’t just about knowing you have money. It’s about knowing what to do with it, what to ask about it and how to make it work for you.
