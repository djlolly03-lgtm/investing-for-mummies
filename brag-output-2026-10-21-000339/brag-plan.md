# Brag Plan: Investing for Mummies

## What is this app?
Money and investing classes for women in Mumbai, taught entirely through games — plus a
new website where all forty of those games are free to play without signing up.

## The angle
A finance course whose proof is its own syllabus. The lessons are not called "Module 3:
Debt Instruments", they are called **NAV Pizza**, **Debt Detective**, **ChaiBoss Ltd** and
**Wheel of Gold** — and one of them asks whether you should marry an investment or run.
The video earns that reveal with a real student's line first: twenty years inside a bank,
still not understanding most of it. The joke is not on her. It is on how finance is
normally taught. Everything on screen is the project's real copy, real classroom and real
game UI.

Deliberately NOT the same video as `brag-output/` (18 Oct), which covered the games
homepage and its question picker. This one is about the course and the room.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Cream paper. One line of navy Lora italic, big: **"Twenty years working at a bank."**
Beat. Then: **"I never understood what most departments did."** A real student quote from
the site's feedback wall, anonymous there and anonymous here. It buys the next 18 seconds
because every viewer knows that feeling and nobody expects a finance ad to open by
admitting it.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The classroom photo arriving beside the line **"Every lesson is a game first"** — the
  site's own H2, with Hiral mid-sentence at the "Invisible Enemy: Inflation" slide.
- The real **Swayamvar** game screen ("Meet the Suitors") with its actual description:
  *should you marry this investment, keep it as a maybe, or run?*
- Five real game names ticking in on the beat: NAV Pizza · Debt Detective · ChaiBoss Ltd ·
  Wheel of Gold · Hidden Fortunes. Nothing invented; this is the published library list.

## Outro / punchline
**"Forty of them. Free to play."** → logo → `investingformummies.com`.
The restraint is the point: no price, no urgency, no "book now". The course sells itself
if the games land.

## User flow worth showing
Entry → key action → result, as the site itself describes it (01/02/03 "How it works"):
1. **You arrive as you are** — no prep, no prior knowledge (the room photo).
2. **One idea, then you play it** — the concept taught, then turned into a game on the
   spot (the Swayamvar screen, which is what the room is actually looking at).
3. **You leave able to decide** — carried by the student quote that opens the film rather
   than restated at the end.
The centrepiece scenes are 2 and 3: real class, real game UI.

## Tone
- Preset: `polished`
- Creative direction: a warm launch film that lets the real game names do the joking
- Interpretation: few scenes, long holds, soft crossfades. Confidence through restraint.
  The only "fast" thing in the video is the syllabus ticking in. No zooms, no flashes, no
  exclamation marks. Warmth comes from the photography and the cream paper, not from motion.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 21s

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: `#f7faf9` (cream) with `#e0f3f0` (mint-lt) as the secondary surface
- Accent: `#2a9d8f` (teal); `#c9a84c` (gold) for the single emphasis moment
- Text: `#1a3a5c` (navy), `#5a7d8a` (muted) for eyebrows
- Display font: Lora (600, italic for quotes)
- Body font: Nunito (700/800 for labels and names)
- Strongest visual element: the portrait-format classroom photographs on cream, used as
  editorial cards rather than full-bleed — they are 788x1400 because the site crops them
  vertically, and framing them deliberately reads better than cropping them to landscape.

## Share copy (draft)
A money class for women where the lessons are called NAV Pizza, Debt Detective and
ChaiBoss Ltd. All forty games are now free to play.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed, sparse motion-matched accents
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-10` (110 BPM, 60s) — the warmest of the five
  bundled tracks and the only one with a strong cue at 0.27s, so the hook lands on the
  first beat instead of after it.
- Music treatment: in from 0.0 at moderate level, duck slightly under the two quote lines
  so they read as speech, lift for the syllabus run, fade out over the last 1.2s.
- Music cue guidance: preset cue file read (`assets/music/cues/...vol-10...json`).
  Strong cues to target: **0.27** (hook line 1), **3.55** (hook line 2), **4.10** (photo
  card in), **7.92** (game card in), **18.01** (URL).
  Beat-grid window for the sequential syllabus: **11.46 / 12.56 / 13.64 / 14.73 / 15.82** —
  every other beat, 1.09s apart, which clears the ~0.8s readable floor for a short label.
- Audio-reactive treatment: none. This tone does not want anything pulsing.
- SFX posture: sparse. At most five: two soft marks under the quote lines, one soft card
  arrival, light ticks on the syllabus names, one quiet settle on the logo.
- Audio-coupled moments: the syllabus names arriving one by one; the photo and game cards
  sliding in; the final logo settle.
- Restraint rule: no whooshes, no risers, no impacts. Nothing that would feel like an ad.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The admission — 4.1s
Cream field. Small muted eyebrow, letterspaced: `FROM A STUDENT, IN HER OWN WORDS`.
Then navy Lora italic, large, two lines arriving separately:
*"Twenty years working at a bank."* (0.27) — holds ~3.0s
*"I never understood what most departments did."* (3.55) — holds ~1.4s into the cut
Sequential/interaction: yes — two quote lines, each slamming in over ~0.35s then holding.
Audio intent: quiet and honest; the music present but low so the lines read as speech.
Audio-coupled idea: a soft mark under each line arrival. No typing effect — this is a
quote, not a terminal.
Music: warm, low.
Transition mood: soft → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — The room — 3.8s
`room-inflation.jpg` slides in from the right as a rounded portrait card with a soft
shadow, occupying the right third. Left side, navy on cream:
small teal eyebrow `SO WE STOPPED EXPLAINING IT` then the site's own line in Lora 600:
**"Every lesson is a game first."**
Sequential/interaction: yes — photo card in at 4.10, headline settles by ~6.0, holds to cut.
Audio intent: the film opens up; warmth arrives with the photograph.
Audio-coupled idea: one soft card-arrival sound as the photo lands.
Music: lifts slightly.
Transition mood: soft crossfade → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — The game — 3.5s
`swayamvar-suitors.jpg` (the real "Meet the Suitors" screen) scales up gently on mint-lt,
framed like a device. Over/under it, Nunito 800 navy:
**"Should you marry this investment, keep it as a maybe, or run?"**
Small gold label: `SWAYAMVAR · PLAYED ON YOUR PHONE, DEBATED IN THE ROOM`
Sequential/interaction: yes — card in at 7.92, line reveals just after and holds ~1.8s
(13 words, so it needs its full floor — this is the longest read in the film).
Audio intent: playful without becoming a toy; the one moment allowed a smile.
Audio-coupled idea: card arrival; no sound on the text.
Music: full.
Transition mood: clean → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — The syllabus — 5.6s
Cream. Eyebrow: `THE SYLLABUS`. Five real game names tick in one per strong cue, stacked
or in a loose two-column arrangement, Nunito 800 navy with a teal rule:
`NAV Pizza` (11.46) · `Debt Detective` (12.56) · `ChaiBoss Ltd` (13.64) ·
`Wheel of Gold` (14.73) · `Hidden Fortunes` (15.82)
All five hold together on screen for ~0.8s after the last arrives.
Sequential/interaction: yes — five names, one per every-other-beat, each held 1.09s.
Audio intent: momentum; the list is the joke and the proof at once.
Audio-coupled idea: a light tick per name, quiet enough to stay under the music.
Music: full, driving.
Transition mood: soft → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — The offer — 4.0s
Cream. Centred. Gold hairline above.
**"Forty of them. Free to play."** (Lora 600)
`logo-round-clear.png` settles above, then `investingformummies.com` in Nunito 700 teal
at 18.01. Holds to 21.0 with a 1.2s music fade.
Sequential/interaction: yes — line, then logo, then URL.
Audio intent: land softly, resolve, stop. No final stinger.
Audio-coupled idea: one quiet settle under the logo.
Music: fades out over the last 1.2s.

**Music mood for this video:** upbeat but warm — a business-moves bed at 110 BPM held
deliberately low under the quote and lifted for the syllabus.
**Audio summary:** quiet and confessional for four seconds, opening into warmth as the
room appears, driving through the syllabus run, then resolving to a soft stop on the URL.
