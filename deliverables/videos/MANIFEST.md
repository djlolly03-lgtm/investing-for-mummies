**Every finished, shareable video lives here.** Working files, build attempts and
raw footage do not.

## Where the originals are

Files here are **copies**. The originals stay where the build scripts and the live
site expect them:

- game reels + teasers -> `CLAUDE/content/reels/`, `CLAUDE/content/teasers/`
  (published URLs — **never move or rename anything under `CLAUDE/`**)
- nursery rhymes -> `Nursery Rhymes/<song>/`
- raw class footage -> `highlight-reel-assets/v####.mov` (sources, deliberately not here)

## Rule

Finished output only. If it is a build attempt, an intermediate, or raw footage,
it does not belong in this folder.
