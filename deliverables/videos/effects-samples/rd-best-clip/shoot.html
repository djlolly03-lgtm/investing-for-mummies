Phone on a tripod, filmed from behind

87 glass-slam · 88 hand-from-screen · 89 zoom-wheel

One set covers three effects. Shoot all three in one session.

87 glass-slam: Two cameras. Camera A (Canon, the hero shot): vertical, on a tripod, ~60 cm behind a phone that is itself on a small tripod; frame so the phone fills ~70% of the height with the room visible around it, focus on the phone screen. The phone (camera B): iPhone in video mode, rear camera facing Hiral, screen facing camera A, brightness at max, Do Not Disturb on. Set: a homely room (bed, sofa or plants), warm light, phone ~1.2 m off the floor. Action: Hiral starts 3-4 m back from the phone, centred. On 'action' she walks briskly toward it (2 s), then leans in and presses both open palms and her face (cheek or nose) flat onto the phone screen, holds 1 s, smiles, pulls back. She can laugh. Total ~5 s. Line, optional, as she pulls back: 'Okay, now I have your attention.' Takes: 5, including one with only palms (no face) as a safe option. Clean the phone glass between takes. Avoid knocking the phone tripod (weigh it down), ceiling lights reflecting on the screen, and walking off-centre.

88 hand-from-screen: Same set as glass slam: Canon vertical on a tripod behind and slightly above a phone on a small tripod, phone filling ~70% of frame height, warm homely room, phone screen at max brightness showing its camera view of Hiral. Hiral sits cross-legged or on a chair ~1 m in front of the phone, centred, framed mid-shot on the phone screen. Action: she talks to the phone for 2 s, then reaches her right arm straight forward toward the phone so her hand (in real life) comes past the phone's edge, into Camera A's view in front of the screen, and her index finger taps the red Record button on the phone screen. Hold the tap 0.5 s, pull the hand back, keep talking. Line before the reach: 'Let me start this for you.' Takes: 5. Do one with her arm coming from directly behind the phone so the hand appears to emerge from the screen. Avoid sleeves covering the hand, the arm entering from the side of the frame, and shaking the phone on the tap (use a heavy tripod).

89 zoom-wheel: Same phone-on-tripod set (Canon behind the phone, phone filling ~70% of frame height, warm room). The phone runs its real camera app with Hiral on screen. Shoot 1 (the real thing): Hiral sits ~1.5 m from the phone; an off-screen helper's (or Hiral's own) hand comes in from the bottom of Camera A's frame, long-presses the '1x' button so the iPhone zoom wheel fans out, and slowly drags it from 0.5x to ~5x over 2 s while Hiral leans in slightly and widens her eyes. Hold 1 s at the close-up. Line at the close-up: 'Now that's a close-up on your money.' Takes: 5, varying drag speed. Shoot 2: the same with the hand only (Hiral seated, no dialogue) as a spare. Avoid a dim phone screen (max brightness), reflections, and the hand covering the dial numbers.

Seated at a clear desk

35 keycap-rain-land

35 keycap-rain-land: Camera: phone on a tripod, vertical 9:16, 1x lens (no zoom), lens at Hiral's chest height, about 1.5 m away. Framing: Hiral seated at a desk, from the top of her head (leave a hand's width of space above it) down to the desk; at least the front 25-30 cm of a CLEAR, empty desk surface visible across the bottom fifth of the frame (move papers, laptop and books off). Background: plain wall or tidy shelf, soft light from a window to one side, no direct sun. Wardrobe: solid colour top (no stripes). Action: 1) Look at camera and say the line for about 4 seconds (e.g. 'SIP, budget, Nifty... all these words keep landing on you.'). 2) On 'landing', look down at the desk for one beat, then back up. 3) Keep hands in her lap or at the sides of the desk, not in the centre, so the keys have room to land. Record 3 takes, plus 5 seconds of the empty desk and chair with no one in it (do not move the camera). Avoid: camera moving between takes, objects on the desk, leaning on the desk.

Tap the air on 'follow'

47 tap

47 tap: Camera: phone, vertical 9:16, on tripod at her eye height, 1 m away, 1x lens. Frame: head to waist, centred, plain wall or sofa behind, soft window light from the front. Hiral talks to camera for 2 s, then on the word 'follow' raises her right index finger and taps the air firmly once at shoulder height, about 25 cm to the right of her body (her right), holds the finger there for 0.5 s, smiles, drops the hand and keeps talking for 2 s. Line: 'If this helped... hit follow.' with the tap on 'follow'. Get 5 takes; keep the tap point in the same spot each take. Avoid: tapping in front of her face, fast blurred gestures, a dark top that hides the hand.

Face camera, small side moves

56 locked-on

56 locked-on: Camera: phone, vertical, tripod at her eye height, 1.2 m away, 1x lens. Frame: head to waist, centred, plain or softly lit background. Hiral faces the lens the whole time. She talks naturally for 5 s and makes two small moves: at 1 s she leans 15 cm to her left, at 3 s she drifts 15 cm back to the right, so the camera has something to follow. Line: 'Hi, I'm Hiral, and I teach money like a game.' Get 4 takes. Avoid: turning to profile, walking out of frame, hair across the face.

A room with real depth

84 vertigo

84 vertigo: Camera: Canon (or phone), vertical, on a tripod at Hiral's eye height, ~1.5-2 m away, 35 mm-equivalent. Location: a room with real DEPTH behind her, at least 4 m of visible space (a corridor, a classroom with rows of chairs, or a living room with a bookshelf and window at the far end). Not the patterned wallpaper. Even light on her face, background a little darker. Shoot 1: Hiral stands still, talking to camera, for 8 s; say the line in the edit ('That's when the whole picture changes') once in the middle. Shoot 2: empty room plate, same camera and framing, 5 s with Hiral out of shot. Best version: a real dolly. Put the tripod on a rolling chair/trolley and pull back 1.5 m while zooming in (or walk the phone back) so her face stays the same size; 3 takes. Avoid moving her head a lot, a flat wall behind her, and backlight from a window behind her.

What we learned

  1. No faces in the background. The effects lock onto the biggest face they find. On the Clear Tax shot a painted face on the wall took over and pushed the comment sheet off screen. Posters, paintings and TVs showing people all count.
  2. Shoot 2 seconds of the empty frame. Dolly-zoom, head-stack and blow-away show what is behind her. Without a real empty plate that area is guessed, and it smears (36 to 60% of it on our phone takes). Only the Canon tripod shoot had one.
  3. Keep the first second still. Three takes started with a handheld jump or a pan from the table. Every effect starts at 0.4 s, so a wobble there ruins it. Start talking, then hold the camera still.
  4. Headroom decides what fits above the head. Comment sheets, toggles, search bars and headlines need a quarter of the frame free above her hair. A tight chest-up shot pushes them onto her head.
  5. Thin lines need a plain wall. Pencil words, the text ring and backdrop words disappear into the jungle wallpaper. On the plain white wall they read at full contrast.
  6. Logos orbit, pictures carousel. Orbit tiles are small and moving: use bold single logos. Carousel cards are big and slow down: use reel covers and posts with text. Carousel spin at turns 0.55.
  7. Off-centre helps the tunnel, but not enough. Rabbit hole improved on the off-centre boardroom shot and still crosses her face twice. It needs her on a third line of the frame. It is the one effect still at 8.5.
  8. Phone takes are HDR. iPhone footage is 4K HLG. Convert it with avconvert first or skin goes grey and flat. 4K also keeps punch-ins and slow pushes sharp.
