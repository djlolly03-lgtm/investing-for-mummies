## Post copy (ready to paste)

```text
Radha's salary comes in on the 1st.
By the 20th, she isn't sure where it went.

So we gave her three buckets.

Emergency: money for now. For the surprise bill. Easy to reach, steady value.
Stability: money for soon. For goals a few years away. Steadier, lower risk.
Growth: money for later. For goals far away. Can grow more, and can swing more.

Radha's first step isn't picking a product. It's writing down what each bucket is for, in rupees and years.

Then: protect before you fill (health and life cover first), give each bucket a different kind of home, and review once a year, not every day.

Which bucket is the emptiest in your life right now?

Educational content, not financial advice.

#Investingformummies #FinancialEducation #FinancialLiteracy #Radhasmoneymission
```
