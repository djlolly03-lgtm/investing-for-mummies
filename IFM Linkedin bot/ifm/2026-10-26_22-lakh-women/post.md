22 lakh women started investing in mutual funds in FY26.

That's not a niche. That's a wave.

A few more numbers worth knowing:
• 1.32 crore women investors in CAMS-serviced funds as of March 2026
• Women's SIP assets have grown about 4x since March 2021, to ₹4.73 lakh crore

Behind every number is someone who once said "I don't really understand this stuff" and started anyway.

What we see in our workshops: women rarely lack the ability. What they lack is a place to start that doesn't make them feel behind.

So here's our question: what made YOU start, or what's still holding you back?
