Happy Children's Day! 🎈

A question that splits every parents' WhatsApp group: should kids know what their parents earn?

Side A: Tell them. Real numbers teach value, trade-offs, and why we sometimes say no.
Side B: Shield them. Children shouldn't carry adult money worries. Teach the concepts, not the numbers.

Wherever you land, remember that silence teaches something too.

Three money conversations you can have this week, at any age:
1. Pocket money as a mini budget: needs, wants and saving
2. Say "can we afford it?" out loud, with your reasoning
3. Why waiting pays: a little saved regularly grows over time

For parents thinking long term, NPS Vatsalya lets you open a pension account for a child. Check the latest rules on the PFRDA website.

Did your parents talk money with you? Will you with yours?

#Investingformummies #FinancialEducation #FinancialLiteracy #ChildrensDay #Parenting
