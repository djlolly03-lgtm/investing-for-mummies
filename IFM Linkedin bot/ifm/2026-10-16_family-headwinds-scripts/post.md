## Post copy (ready to paste)

```text
"Ladies don't need to worry about all this."

We asked our mums which line they'd heard most when they tried to talk about money at home. This one came up again and again.

Let's name the headwind kindly. It's usually not malice. It's protectiveness, habit, "log kya kahenge", and a fear that money talk will turn into a fight.

But silence has a cost. It leaves women helpless at exactly the moment they most need to know.

Here are five lines women in our batches have used to open the door (all in the carousel):

1. "If something happened to you, I'd want to know where to start. Can we make a list together?"
2. "Can you walk me through one thing a month? Just one."
3. "I'm learning about investing. Can I practise on our own numbers?"
4. "Let's check our policies and nominees together, so we both know."
5. "What are we actually saving for, ten years from now?"

Start small: one document, one account, one question.

And if the door stays shut, learn on your own first, so the next conversation starts from confidence.

Which line would work in your home, or what worked for you?

Educational content, not financial advice.

#Investingformummies #FinancialEducation #FinancialLiteracy #WomenAndMoney #FamilyMoney
```
