Regular plan or direct plan?

It's one tick on a mutual fund form, and many investors never realise it was there.

Same fund. Same fund manager. Same portfolio. The difference is how you buy it.

📄 Regular plan: bought through a distributor. Their commission is built into the fund's yearly costs (the expense ratio).
💻 Direct plan: bought directly from the fund house or a direct platform. No distributor commission, so a lower expense ratio.

Neither is "wrong". A good distributor can add real value through guidance and hand-holding. But you should know which one you're choosing, and why.

4 questions to ask anyone helping you invest:
1. Are you a SEBI-registered investment adviser, or a mutual fund distributor?
2. How are you paid, and by whom?
3. Is this a regular or a direct plan, and why?
4. Can you explain it in words I could repeat to someone else?

Have you ever checked which plan your funds are in? Tell us in the comments.
