Buying gold this Dhanteras? Here are 4 ways to own it, and they follow very different rules.

1. Physical gold: jewellery, coins, bars. Look for the BIS hallmark. Jewellery adds making charges.
2. Gold ETFs: track the gold price, SEBI-regulated, bought on the exchange (needs a demat account).
3. Gold mutual funds: usually invest in gold ETFs for you. No demat needed, and SIPs are possible.
4. "Digital gold" on apps: convenient, but NOT regulated by SEBI, so check who stores it and the fees.

And Sovereign Gold Bonds? There have been no new issues since Feb 2024. Existing ones trade on the exchange, and Budget 2026 changed the tax treatment, so check the current rules.

Save this before Friday. Which one did you not know about?
