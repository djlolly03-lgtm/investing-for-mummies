```text
Women entrepreneurs in our community often tell us the hardest part of starting out wasn't the idea.

It was discovering how much of the practical side sat with someone else: the CA, the bank relationship, the paperwork, the logins.

So here's a checklist we wish every woman had before starting a business. 5 things to have in your own name:

✅ A bank account with your own net-banking login
✅ Copies of your key documents: PAN, Aadhaar, property and policy papers
✅ A direct line to the CA, not only through someone else
✅ Your own credit history, and a check of your credit score
✅ The numbers of your bank RM, insurance agent and adviser

None of this is about distrust. These roles usually landed with someone for convenience. But when you're building something of your own, convenience can quietly become dependence.

Women entrepreneurs: what would you add to this list? And what was the hardest part of starting for you? Your story might be exactly what someone needs to read today.

Educational content, not financial advice.
```
