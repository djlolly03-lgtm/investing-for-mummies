If something happened to your husband tomorrow, would you know where to start?

Every bank account.
Every insurance policy.
Who the nominee is, on each one.
Where the will is kept.
The CA's and the adviser's numbers.

Most of us couldn't tick all five. Should we be able to?

Side A: Privacy matters. Trust doesn't mean full access to every login.
Side B: In an emergency, the partner who doesn't know is left helpless at the worst possible moment.

A middle path many couples use: not sharing passwords, but keeping one updated "if anything happens" list in a safe place that both partners know about.

Where do you stand? Vote in the poll in the comments, or tell us how your family handles it.
