```text
"Why are you asking? Don't you trust me?"

Many women in our batches tell us a simple money question at home has been met with some version of this.

Neither side is the villain. For many men, managing the family's money is how they show care, so a question can feel like an audit. And for many women, the question is about wanting to help, not wanting to check.

Here are 3 ways to ask that land better:

1. Lead with the why.
"If anything happens, I want to be able to help."

2. Ask to learn, not to check.
"Can you walk me through how our investments are set up?"

3. Make it a routine, not an event.
A 20-minute money date each month, instead of a one-off grilling on the day the statement arrives.

It's teamwork, not a trust test.

Which of these would work in your home? And to the husbands reading: how would you want to be asked?
```
