Before you do, meet Miss Gold Malhotra.

She's one of six suitors in Swayamvar, the game we play in class to teach asset allocation. Her biodata says it all: "Family favourite since 3000 BC." Grandma approves. Mom approves. Bua definitely approves.

But every biodata has red flags. Hers? "Sometimes just sits there looking pretty." The gold in your locker doesn't pay interest, rent or dividends. That isn't her job. She's the one the family trusts when times get rough.

In the game, everyone votes on their phone. Who would you marry? Who would you date? Who would you call at 2 AM? And the loudest round of all: who would your mother choose? (Guess who Mum picks.)

Then comes the twist. Nobody gets just one suitor. Every suitor gets a role, and Miss Gold goes into the Stability bucket, next to Debt and Real Estate.

The secret to wealth isn't finding The One. It's knowing what each one is for.

So buy your gold with joy tomorrow. Just know her role.
