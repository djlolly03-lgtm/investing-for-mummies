Women know where every document in the house is. Except the investing document.

We manage the house, the schedules, the family calendar. Half the time, the family itself.

But when someone says "mutual funds," many of us still say "my husband will handle it."

So here's this week's debate:

Side A: Wealth belongs to whoever earns it. If he earns, it's his to manage.
Side B: If we're helping build this life (our work, our sacrifices, our unpaid labour), then this is our family's wealth too.

I'm firmly on Side B. That doesn't mean taking over. It means being in the room when the decisions are made.

You don't need to become a finance expert overnight. You just need to start participating.

Is it "his money" or "our money" in your home? I'd genuinely love to hear both sides.
