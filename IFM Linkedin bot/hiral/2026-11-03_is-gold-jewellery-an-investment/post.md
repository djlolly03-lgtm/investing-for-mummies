Is gold jewellery an investment?

Ask this at any family dinner during Diwali week and watch what happens. 😄

Side A: Yes. It holds value, you can wear it, and it has been the family's safety net for generations. Our mothers and grandmothers relied on it.

Side B: Not really. Making charges (and often wastage) are paid upfront and rarely come back. And the pieces we love are the ones we'd never sell.

My honest answer? It's both. An heirloom first, an investment second. And that's okay, as long as we know which one we're buying.

One practical tip either way: ask for the break-up on your bill, gold value vs making charges. You'll know exactly what you're paying for beauty and what you're paying for gold.

Where do you stand: investment, heirloom, or both?
