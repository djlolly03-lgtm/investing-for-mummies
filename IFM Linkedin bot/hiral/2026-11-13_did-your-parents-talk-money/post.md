Tomorrow is Children's Day, and I've been thinking about the money lessons we pass on without saying a word.

Kids notice who pays the bills. Who opens the bank letters. Who says "ask your father" when money comes up. Who goes quiet.

Many of us grew up in homes where money simply wasn't discussed in front of children. It was meant kindly. But it also meant many of us reached adulthood without the vocabulary.

I don't think children need to carry adult money worries. But I do think they should see both parents comfortable with money.

So my question for parents: did your parents talk money with you? And will you with yours?

Tomorrow the IFM page is sharing three easy money conversations you can have with children of any age.
