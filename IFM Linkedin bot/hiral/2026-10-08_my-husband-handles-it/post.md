```text
For years, "my husband handles it" was my answer to every money question.

I said it proudly. Today I think it was the riskiest sentence in our home.

I spent ten years as a business journalist at Business India. I wrote about markets and companies for a living.
And yet my husband and our planner handled our family's money. I only took charge after my second child.

Losing my father early taught me what happens when only one person knows where everything is.

So, the debate:

Side A: Dividing the work is sensible. He handles money, I handle a hundred other things. That's partnership.
Side B: Delegating is fine; not knowing isn't. You can let him do the investing, but you should know what exists, where it is and why.

I'm firmly in Side B. Not because of distrust. Because two adults should both be able to steer.

Is "my husband handles it" a sensible division of labour, or a risk we've normalised?
```
