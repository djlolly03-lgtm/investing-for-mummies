## Post copy (ready to paste)

```text
Wife. Daughter. Mother. Caretaker. Homemaker. Doctor. Nurturer.

I've worn most of these hats myself, often on the same day. So have most women I know.

So here's what I keep wondering: why does "financial" still feel like someone else's hat?

Look at what the other hats already involve.

The homemaker runs the monthly budget and knows what everything costs.
The mother plans years ahead for school, college and health.
The caretaker handles hospital bills, insurance calls and the paperwork nobody else wants.
The daughter is often the one managing her parents' care and documents.

That is financial work. We just don't call it that.

I'm not judging anyone here. I spent ten years as a business journalist, and I still said "my husband handles it" about our own money.

So here's the debate I'd love your view on:

Side A: One person handling the money is efficient. One tracks it, the other trusts.
Side B: Every hat-wearer deserves a seat when the big decisions are made.

Most homes quietly choose Side A, until the day that one person isn't available.

I believe women already have the ability. What's missing is the vocabulary, and the seat at the table.

Which hat do you wear most? And is "financial" one of them yet?

#Investingformummies #FinancialEducation #FinancialLiteracy #WomenAndMoney #Mothers
```
