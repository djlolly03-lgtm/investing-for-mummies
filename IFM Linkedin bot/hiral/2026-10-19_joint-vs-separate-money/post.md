One joint account for everything? Or "yours, mine and ours"?

Every couple I know has a strong opinion about this, and most have never actually discussed it.

Side A (joint): One pot, one team. Total transparency. Simpler to plan.
Side B (separate): Independence, no guilt over personal spends, and protection if life changes.

The hybrid many couples land on: a joint pot for shared goals, plus money in each person's own name.

I loved how a participant in our last batch described her approach. Her husband takes more risk with his investments, so her plan is to "balance him out" with the stability bucket. "Now I have a little arsenal with my husband."

Not his money or her money. A team with two strategies.

My view: whatever system you choose, every woman should have something in her own name.

Which system does your home run on, and would you change it?

Educational content, not financial advice.
