"Sorry, this is probably a silly question…"

I hear it in every batch. Usually right before the smartest question of the day.

The idea for Investing for Mummies came on a girls' trip, when brilliant, capable friends quietly admitted they didn't really understand their own money. And every one of them apologised before saying so.

Think about it. We were taught maths, science, even baking.
But we weren't taught how to invest.

Not knowing something you were never taught is not a failure.

Side A: The apology is just politeness. Harmless.
Side B: It signals we believe money isn't "our" subject, and that belief keeps us out of the room.

I'm with Side B. So ask the question. Ask the "stupid" question. And if you don't understand, ask again.

When did you last apologise before asking a money question, and who were you asking?

#Investingformummies #FinancialEducation #FinancialLiteracy #WomenSupportingWomen #MoneyTalk
