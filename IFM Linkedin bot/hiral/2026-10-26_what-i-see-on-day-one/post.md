On the first day of every batch, I see the same thing.

Women who run households, teams, businesses and family calendars, sitting with their notebooks closed. A little worried they'll be "the only one who doesn't know."

By the third session the notebooks are open.
By the last one, they're explaining compounding to each other, and then to their husbands at dinner.

Nobody became "good with money" in between. They just finally had a room where it was safe to ask.

That's the part of this work I love most.

Tomorrow the IFM page is sharing a look inside our workshops: the handbook, the games, the questions.

Have you ever been in a room where you were scared to ask? What finally helped you speak up?
