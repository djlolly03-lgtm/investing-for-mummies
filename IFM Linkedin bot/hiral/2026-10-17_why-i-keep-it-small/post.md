People often ask me why I keep our batches to 10-12 women.

Because the most important question in the room is usually the one someone almost didn't ask.

In a big hall, that question stays quiet. In a room of twelve, around one table, with a printed handbook and a few games, it finally comes out. And usually three other women were wondering exactly the same thing.

I know that feeling well. I wrote about markets for a living as a business journalist, and I still didn't fully understand our own family's money. Nobody had made it simple, or safe to ask.

That's what our eight sessions at Nariman Point try to be: simple, and safe to ask.

Our next batch starts Tuesday, 17 November. Early-bird registration closes this Tuesday, 20 October.
