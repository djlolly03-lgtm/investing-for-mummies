How hard is it for a woman to actually start a business today?

On paper, easier than ever. Schemes, loans, online stores, Instagram.

But in many homes, the practical things are "handled":
the CA is his CA.
The bank relationship manager calls him.
The property papers, the GST login, the company forms all sit with the husband, or with staff.

So when a woman wants to start something of her own, what's really in her way?

Is it money? Getting capital without a co-signer or a family guarantee.
Is it confidence? Feeling she has to "prove" it's a real business before anyone takes it seriously.
Or is it who holds the paperwork, the bank relationships and the know-how?

My guess is it's often the third, and it's the easiest to fix. Open an account in your own name. Meet the CA yourself. Know where every document is kept.

No one is the villain here. These roles usually came about for convenience. But convenience can quietly become dependence.

Women entrepreneurs: what was the hardest part of starting for you? Share your story below. Someone reading it is about to begin.
