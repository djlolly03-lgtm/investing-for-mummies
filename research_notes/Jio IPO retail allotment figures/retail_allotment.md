# Jio Platforms IPO — Retail Investor Allotment Figures

## IPO Basics: Lot Size and Issue Price

### Takeaway
The Jio Platforms IPO priced at ₹1,119 per share (upper end of ₹1,065–₹1,119 band), with a lot size of 12 shares, making the minimum retail investment ₹13,428.

### Cited Findings
- Price band: ₹1,065 to ₹1,119 per share — [Chittorgarh](https://www.chittorgarh.com/ipo/jio-platforms-ipo/3152/); [Asset Master](https://assetmaster.in/blogs/reliance-jio-ipo-2026-price-band-1-065-1-119-dates-lot-size-gmp-should-you-apply)
- Issue price fixed at ₹1,119 (upper end) — [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/markets/stocks/story/jio-platforms-ipo-issue-price-fixed-at-rs-1119-per-share-allotment-listing-dates-and-gmp-563526-2026-10-26)
- Lot size: 12 shares — [Chittorgarh](https://www.chittorgarh.com/ipo/jio-platforms-ipo/3152/); [IPO Watch](https://ipowatch.in/jio-platforms-ipo/); [IPO Ji](https://www.ipoji.com/ipo/jio-platforms-ipo)
- Retail minimum application: 1 lot = 12 shares = ₹13,428 — [Chittorgarh](https://www.chittorgarh.com/ipo/jio-platforms-ipo/3152/)
- Retail maximum application: 14 lots = 168 shares = ₹1,87,992 — [Chittorgarh](https://www.chittorgarh.com/ipo/jio-platforms-ipo/3152/)
- IPO open: Oct 21–23, 2026; allotment finalized: Oct 26, 2026; listing: Oct 28, 2026 — [Chittorgarh](https://www.chittorgarh.com/ipo/jio-platforms-ipo/3152/)
- Total fresh issue: up to ~27 crore shares, raising ~₹30,213 crore at upper band — [Asset Master](https://assetmaster.in/blogs/reliance-jio-ipo-2026-price-band-1-065-1-119-dates-lot-size-gmp-should-you-apply)

### Inferences
- Retail maximum cap of 14 lots (₹1.88 lakh) is consistent with SEBI's ₹2 lakh retail ceiling.

### Gaps
- Exact total issue size in crore shares not confirmed from primary (RHP/SEBI filing) source.


## How Many Retail Applicants Applied?

### Takeaway
The IPO drew over 1.38 crore total applications across all categories — a record for Indian IPOs — but the retail-specific application count is not separately disclosed in any source found.

### Cited Findings
- Total applications across all categories: 1,38,65,863 — [Chittorgarh](https://www.chittorgarh.com/ipo/jio-platforms-ipo/3152/)
- Over 1 crore applications cited as a record, beating Waaree Energies' previous record of 97.34 lakh — [IPO Guru](https://www.ipoguru.in/news/jio-platforms-ipo-1-crore-applications-record) (citing Economic Times)
- Retail shares offered: 8,50,37,326 (35% of net issue) — [Chittorgarh](https://www.chittorgarh.com/ipo/jio-platforms-ipo/3152/)
- Maximum retail allottees (i.e., max number who could get 1 lot each): 70,86,443 — [Chittorgarh](https://www.chittorgarh.com/ipo/jio-platforms-ipo/3152/); [IPO Ji](https://www.ipoji.com/ipo/jio-platforms-ipo)

### Inferences
- The 1.38 crore total applications includes QIB, NII (HNI), retail, employee, and shareholder categories. Retail applicants are a subset of this. With retail subscribed ~2.72x and 70.86 lakh maximum allottees, the implied retail application count is approximately 70.86 × 2.72 ≈ 1.93 crore applications in the retail category — but this is an inference, not a published figure.

### Gaps
- Retail-only application count not published in any source found; would need the KFin Technologies basis-of-allotment document for the exact figure.


## Retail Allotment Rate (% who got shares)

### Takeaway
Retail was oversubscribed ~2.72x, implying roughly 36–37% of retail applicants received an allotment via lottery. This is an inference from the subscription multiple, not a published allotment rate.

### Cited Findings
- Retail subscription (final, Day 3): 2.72x — [IPO Ji](https://www.ipoji.com/ipo/jio-platforms-ipo); [IndMoney](https://www.indmoney.com/blog/ipo/jio-ipo-allotment-status); [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/markets/stocks/story/jio-platforms-ipo-issue-price-fixed-at-rs-1119-per-share-allotment-listing-dates-and-gmp-563526-2026-10-26)
- One source shows retail subscription at 2.76x (Day 3, 7:28 PM snapshot) — [Chittorgarh](https://www.chittorgarh.com/ipo/jio-platforms-ipo/3152/)
- Retail shares bid for: 23,49,11,016 against 8,50,37,326 offered (2.76x implied) — [Chittorgarh](https://www.chittorgarh.com/ipo/jio-platforms-ipo/3152/)
- Maximum retail allottees: 70,86,443 (the ceiling, assuming every allottee gets exactly 1 minimum lot) — [Chittorgarh](https://www.chittorgarh.com/ipo/jio-platforms-ipo/3152/)
- Retail applicant's chance: approximately 37%, allotted by lottery — [IPO Track](https://ipotrack.in/blog/jio-platforms-ipo-allotment-status-how-to-check)
- One conflicting outlier: ipomarket.in cited retail subscription at 1.71x (allotment odds ~58.5%); inconsistent with all other sources — treat with caution

### Inferences
- At 2.72x oversubscription: approximately 1 in 2.7 retail applicants (about 36.8%) gets shares.
- At 2.76x: approximately 1 in 2.76 (about 36.2%) gets shares.
- Allotment is by lottery for the minimum lot — an applicant either gets 1 lot or nothing (SEBI norm for oversubscribed retail categories).

### Gaps
- Exact allotment ratio (e.g., "1 in 3") from the KFin Technologies basis-of-allotment notice was not found in any source. The ~37% estimate is computed from subscription data, not a published allotment statistic.


## Listing Price and Listing Day Performance

### Takeaway
Jio Platforms listed at ₹1,358 on October 28, 2026 — a 21.36% premium over the ₹1,119 issue price — and ended the day near ₹1,417–₹1,418, a ~26.7% gain from issue price.

### Cited Findings
- Listing price (open): ₹1,358.00 on both BSE and NSE — [Chittorgarh](https://www.chittorgarh.com/ipo/jio-platforms-ipo/3152/); [Desh Gujarat](https://deshgujarat.com/2026/10/28/jio-platforms-ipo-listing-record-breaking-issue-debuts-at-21-36-premium/); [IPO Ji](https://www.ipoji.com/ipo/jio-platforms-ipo)
- Listing gain: 21.36% — [Desh Gujarat](https://deshgujarat.com/2026/10/28/jio-platforms-ipo-listing-record-breaking-issue-debuts-at-21-36-premium/); [Multibagg](https://www.multibagg.ai/market-pulse/articles/jio-platforms-ipo-listing-gain-cmvsu770y000s2mo6298mfg80)
- Day high: ₹1,423.80 (BSE); ₹1,423.90 (NSE) — [Chittorgarh](https://www.chittorgarh.com/ipo/jio-platforms-ipo/3152/)
- Day low: ₹1,356.40 (BSE); ₹1,357.00 (NSE) — [Chittorgarh](https://www.chittorgarh.com/ipo/jio-platforms-ipo/3152/)
- Last trade (listing day): ₹1,417.35 (BSE); ₹1,417.50 (NSE) — [Chittorgarh](https://www.chittorgarh.com/ipo/jio-platforms-ipo/3152/)
- Described as "second-best debut among India's 10 largest IPOs" — [Desh Gujarat](https://deshgujarat.com/2026/10/28/jio-platforms-ipo-listing-record-breaking-issue-debuts-at-21-36-premium/)

### Inferences
- Closing price on listing day (~₹1,417) was about 4.4% above the opening listing price of ₹1,358.
- For an applicant who sold at open: gain = ₹1,358 − ₹1,119 = ₹239/share (21.36%).
- For an applicant who held till close: gain = ₹1,417.50 − ₹1,119 = ₹298.50/share (~26.7%).


## What Did a Retail Applicant Who Got 1 Lot Make?

### Takeaway
A retail investor allotted the minimum lot of 12 shares made ₹2,868 in notional listing gain (selling at the opening price of ₹1,358). If they held till end of listing day (~₹1,417.50), the gain was approximately ₹3,582.

### Cited Findings
- Notional gain on 1 minimum lot (12 shares) at listing open: ₹2,868 — [Desh Gujarat](https://deshgujarat.com/2026/10/28/jio-platforms-ipo-listing-record-breaking-issue-debuts-at-21-36-premium/); [IPO Ji](https://www.ipoji.com/ipo/jio-platforms-ipo) (₹2,868 = 12 × (₹1,358 − ₹1,119))
- Listing price used for this calculation: ₹1,358 — [Chittorgarh](https://www.chittorgarh.com/ipo/jio-platforms-ipo/3152/)
- Investment amount per lot: ₹13,428 (12 × ₹1,119) — [Chittorgarh](https://www.chittorgarh.com/ipo/jio-platforms-ipo/3152/)

### Inferences
- Return on 1 minimum lot at listing open: ₹2,868 / ₹13,428 = 21.36%.
- If sold at day close (~₹1,417.50): gain ≈ ₹3,582 (12 × ₹298.50), a ~26.7% return.
- These are gross/notional gains before brokerage, STT, and other transaction costs.

### Gaps
- No source published actual realized average exit price for retail sellers on listing day.


## What Did a Retail Applicant Who Got Nothing Make?

### Takeaway
An applicant who was not allotted shares (the majority, given ~2.72x oversubscription) received ₹0 profit. Their blocked application amount of ₹13,428 (1 lot) was unblocked and returned, with no interest earned, typically by October 27, 2026.

### Cited Findings
- Refund/unblocking date: October 27, 2026 (one day before listing) — [Chittorgarh](https://www.chittorgarh.com/ipo/jio-platforms-ipo/3152/)
- Applications are via ASBA; money is blocked in the bank account, not debited, unless allotted — standard SEBI rule, referenced across all broker/aggregator sites
- Retail subscription ~2.72x implies roughly 63% of retail applicants were not allotted — computed from subscription data

### Inferences
- Approximately 63% of retail applicants got ₹0 gain and had their funds unblocked on Oct 27.
- No opportunity cost of blocked funds is typically computed in Indian IPO context (ASBA simply freezes the balance; the account continues to earn interest at the bank's normal savings rate).

### Gaps
- Exact count of retail applicants who did not receive allotment — not published; would require KFin basis-of-allotment document.


## What Did the Average HNI (NII) Applicant Get?

### Takeaway
The HNI (NII) categories were significantly more oversubscribed than retail (10–16x vs 2.72x), meaning proportional allotment per applicant was much smaller. At 15.88x for bNII (big HNI), an applicant who applied for ₹10 lakh+ received roughly 6.3% of their bid value in shares. At 10.20x for sNII (small HNI), an applicant received roughly 9.8% of their bid value.

### Cited Findings
- NII total subscription: 13.99x — [Chittorgarh](https://www.chittorgarh.com/ipo/jio-platforms-ipo/3152/)
- bNII (above ₹10 lakh): subscribed 15.88x; 2,42,96,380 shares offered; max allottees 1,34,979 — [Chittorgarh](https://www.chittorgarh.com/ipo/jio-platforms-ipo/3152/); [IPO Ji](https://www.ipoji.com/ipo/jio-platforms-ipo)
- sNII (below ₹10 lakh, min ₹2 lakh): subscribed 10.20x; 1,21,48,189 shares offered; max allottees 67,489 — [Chittorgarh](https://www.chittorgarh.com/ipo/jio-platforms-ipo/3152/); [IPO Ji](https://www.ipoji.com/ipo/jio-platforms-ipo)
- sHNI minimum: 15 lots (180 shares, ₹2,01,420); bHNI minimum: 75 lots (900 shares, ₹10,07,100) — [IPO Watch](https://ipowatch.in/jio-platforms-ipo/)
- Notional listing gain for HNI allotted 180 shares (minimum sNII lot): ₹43,020 — [Desh Gujarat](https://deshgujarat.com/2026/10/28/jio-platforms-ipo-listing-record-breaking-issue-debuts-at-21-36-premium/) (180 × (₹1,358 − ₹1,119) = 180 × ₹239 = ₹43,020)

### Inferences
- NII allotment is proportional (not lottery), so an oversubscribed NII applicant gets a fraction of their bid.
- bNII at 15.88x: an applicant got roughly 1/15.88 ≈ 6.3% of their application amount in shares. On a ₹10,07,100 minimum bid, that is about ₹63,500 worth of shares → listing gain ≈ ₹63,500 × 21.36% ≈ ₹13,565.
- sNII at 10.20x: roughly 1/10.20 ≈ 9.8% of application amount. On a ₹2,01,420 minimum bid, that is about ₹19,750 in shares → listing gain ≈ ₹19,750 × 21.36% ≈ ₹4,219.
- The ₹43,020 "HNI gain" cited is for someone allotted a minimum sNII lot of 180 shares, which assumes near-100% allotment — only possible if sNII oversubscription were near 1x. At actual 10.20x, a minimum sNII applicant likely got far fewer shares.

### Gaps
- Exact allotment per NII applicant not published; NII allotment follows a proportional rather than lottery basis but the precise per-application allotment calculation requires the KFin basis-of-allotment document.
- Whether the ₹43,020 figure from Desh Gujarat is for a minimum-lot sNII application at 1x (hypothetical) or an actual allotment scenario is unclear from that source.
