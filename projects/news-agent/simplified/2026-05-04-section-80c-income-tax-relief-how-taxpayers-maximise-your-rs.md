**Save Tax and Grow Money — Here's How to Do It**

Did you know the government lets you reduce your tax bill just by saving and investing smartly? Under a rule called Section 80C, you can save tax on up to ₹1.5 lakh per year — that's money that stays in your pocket instead of going to the government.

**What this means for you:**
- **Start early in April.** If you invest in PPF (Public Provident Fund — a safe, government-backed savings scheme) before April 5th, your money earns interest for the full year. Wait longer and you lose a month's earnings!
- **Use the full ₹1.5 lakh limit.** You can spread it across different options — for example, ₹50,000 in PPF, ₹50,000 in ELSS (a type of mutual fund with tax benefits), and ₹50,000 for life insurance premiums.
- **Moms, this one's for you!** School fees for up to two children and Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (a savings scheme specially for daughters) also count towards this limit.
- **Want to save even more tax?** Investing ₹50,000 extra in NPS (National Pension Scheme — for your retirement) can push your total tax deduction to ₹2 lakh.

**The bottom line:**
Investing just ₹1.5 lakh wisely each year can reduce your tax bill AND grow your savings — so start early and choose options that match your family's goals.
