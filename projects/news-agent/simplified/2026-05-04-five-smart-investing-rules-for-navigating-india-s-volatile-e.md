**5 Simple Rules to Grow Your Money, Even When Markets Feel Scary**

The stock market can feel like a rollercoaster — going up one day, down the next. But a few simple habits can help you stay calm and build wealth steadily over time.

**What this means for you:**
- **Split your investments wisely.** Put 70% of your money in big, stable companies (think of them like a solid house foundation) and 30% in newer, faster-growing ones. This way, you're protected even when markets dip.
- **Don't put all your eggs in one basket.** Spread your money across different types of businesses — banking, everyday goods, manufacturing. If one area struggles, the others can carry you through, like balancing your family budget across different expenses.
- **Stay invested, don't wait.** Trying to pick the "perfect" moment to invest often means missing out on big gains. Starting a SIP (a fixed monthly investment, like an EMI but for your future) and staying consistent beats waiting for the "right time."
- **Keep your emotions in check.** When markets fall, it feels scary — but don't panic and pull your money out. If you're invested in good companies, a temporary dip is often just a sale, not a disaster.

**The bottom line:**
The secret to growing wealth isn't being the smartest investor — it's being the most patient one.
