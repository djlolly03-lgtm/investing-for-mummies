**Staying Invested Is the Real Secret to Growing Money**

A popular story online recently called a famous fraudster a "genius investor." But the real lesson hidden inside that story is something every beginner investor in India should know — it's not about picking the right investment, it's about *staying* invested long enough to see it grow.

**What this means for you:**
- **Picking is not the hard part — holding is.** Anyone can choose a mutual fund or stock. The real challenge is not panicking and selling when markets fall. Markets will always go up and down — that is normal.
- **Your SIP is your superpower.** A woman who started a ₹10,000 monthly SIP (a fixed monthly investment into a mutual fund) in 2008 survived every market crash since then — and came out ahead. No drama, just patience.
- **Only invest money that is truly yours.** The fraudster in this story lost everything because he was investing *other people's* money illegally. When you invest your own honest savings, nobody can force you to sell at the wrong time.
- **You don't need to find the "next big thing."** Boring, steady, regular investing in simple mutual funds has quietly built real wealth for ordinary Indian families for decades.

**The bottom line:**
Invest your own money regularly, don't touch it, and let time do the heavy lifting — that's the only strategy that truly works.
