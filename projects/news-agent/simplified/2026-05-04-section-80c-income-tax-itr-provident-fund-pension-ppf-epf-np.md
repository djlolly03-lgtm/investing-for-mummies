**Save Tax and Grow Money — Here's How!**

Did you know the government actually rewards you for saving money? Under a rule called Section 80C, you can reduce the amount of income tax you pay — by up to ₹1.5 lakh every year. This only works under the "old tax regime" (the traditional way of filing taxes). Here's what you need to know.

**What this means for you:**
- **PPF (Public Provident Fund)** is a safe, government-backed savings account. Invest up to ₹1.5 lakh yearly, save on tax, AND the interest you earn is completely tax-free. Perfect for long-term goals like your child's education.
- **Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana** is specially for your daughter (under age 10). You invest, she benefits later — and you get a tax break too. A beautiful two-in-one gift.
- **Children's school tuition fees** count! Fees paid for up to two children can be claimed under this rule. So something you're already paying can help reduce your tax bill.
- **Life insurance premiums, EPF contributions, and home loan repayments** also qualify. You may already be saving tax without even realising it!

**The bottom line:**
Check what you're already spending on — school fees, insurance, home loans — because many of these count toward your ₹1.5 lakh tax saving limit, meaning more money stays in your pocket.
