📋 Note for Financial Planners — Methodology

The corpus required at retirement is calculated using a monthly growing annuity (end-of-period) formula — not the annual annuity-due with real return rate commonly used in Excel-based financial plans.

What this means: Each month's withdrawal during retirement grows at inflation ÷ 12 per month. The corpus is assumed to earn the post-retirement return on a monthly compounding basis. Withdrawals happen at end of each month.

Formula used: Corpus = PMT × [1 − ((1+g)/(1+r))ⁿ] ÷ (r − g) where PMT = monthly expense at retirement, r = post-retirement return/12, g = inflation/12, n = post-retirement months.

Vs. Excel FP method: Standard FP practice uses an annual annuity-due with the real return rate [(1+r)/(1+g)−1], treating 12 months of withdrawals as a single beginning-of-year lump. That method produces a corpus ~1.8% higher. Both are valid; this tool's monthly approach is more granular and mathematically precise — the simulated corpus drains to exactly ₹0 by life expectancy.
