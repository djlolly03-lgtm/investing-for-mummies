Think of compounding like a snowball rolling downhill. The earlier you start, the longer the hill — and your snowball gets much bigger.

When you invest ₹10,000/month and earn 12% per year, your money doesn't just add up — it starts earning on its own earnings. By Year 5, your returns are also generating returns.

Waiting 5 years doesn't just mean losing 5 years of savings — it means losing all the compounding those 5 early years would have triggered for the remaining years too.
