So what does this mean for your money?

The chessboard doubles every square — 100% growth per step. The stock market doesn't do that, but good Indian index funds have averaged 12–15% a year for decades.

At 12% a year, money doubles roughly every 6 years. Put away ₹10,000 today and leave it — after 42 years (7 doublings) it becomes ₹12,80,000. Without adding another rupee.

The chessboard's secret isn't the doubling. It's the time. Start early.
