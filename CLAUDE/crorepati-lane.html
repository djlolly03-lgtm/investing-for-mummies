How to play

Goal: Be the last mum standing — grow your money while everyone else goes broke.

Your turn: Tap Roll Dice and your token moves around Crorepati Lane.

Asset spaces (Fixed Income, Gold, Real Estate, Mutual Funds, Stocks) can be bought. Land on someone else's asset and you pay them returns (like rent).

Own a full colour group → returns double, and you can top-up your SIP (build) to boost returns further. 5th top-up = Compounding status 💎.

Market Event 🎲 & Money Mantra 💡 cards shake things up — bull runs, crashes, bonuses, bills.

PAYDAY pays your salary each lap. Market Crash 🐻 freezes you. Dividend Pool 💰 collects all the penalties — land on it to scoop the pot.

Run out of cash and can't pay? You're out. Last mum with money is the Crorepati! 👑
