# Reading this before approving

## What "81 rows" buys, and what it does not

After the plan, every one of the 1,340 files is in one of four states:

| | files |
|---|---|
| individually catalogued (371 today + the new rows' own targets) | ~460 |
| deliberately behind a catch-all — raw session dumps | ~560 |
| deliberately excluded — minors pending consent, PSD sources, RAW duplicates | 10 |
| still needing a human look | 9 |

**Nothing is left silently unreachable.** That is the goal; "everything has its own row" is not.

## The three judgement calls inside this plan

**1. A carousel is one row, not six.** 58 of the 81 rows are carousel and story sets — 332
files. Treating each artboard as an asset would put 332 near-identical slides into a library
that was decluttered for exactly this reason, and it contradicts the rule already written in
CLAUDE.md. If you disagree, this is the number that changes: 58 rows becomes 332.

**2. A reel is one row, its cover is the thumbnail.** 17 rows across 12 folders, 32 files. The
cover PNG is not a separate asset. Two folders hold two reels each, so they get two rows.

**3. Raw session dumps stay catch-all — 400 files across 5 folders**, the largest being
`Rakshita` (170) and `IFM Aug 2026` (101). These already have rows. The moments rule would say
to pull ~20 real moments out of each, and that is worth doing eventually — but it is vision
work on 400 frames, not cataloguing, and it is not what this plan proposes.

## Four folders need a catch-all row that does not exist yet

`Pictures` (121), `Sakshi uploads` (16), `Workshop Pictures` (11), `Hiral Goel` (10). These are
session dumps like the five above; they simply have no row at all, which is why they showed up
as unreachable rather than as catch-all. 4 rows, 158 files.

⚠️ `Pictures` is the one to look at yourself. 121 files, and the frame I sampled is eight women
in an IFM workshop with workbooks — real content, and a candidate for moment extraction rather
than a single catch-all.
