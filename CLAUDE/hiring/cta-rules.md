# The CTA Rule

**For:** the content agency · **From:** Hiral, IFM · **Effective:** immediately, on every post from now on

*Also shared with Sakshi, who applies the same rule to Stories.*

---

## The rule

> **No caption goes out without one clear ask.**

One. Not none, not three. Every post tells the reader exactly what to do next.

### Where we're starting from

Of IFM's last 12 posts, **not one asked the reader to do anything that leads toward a class.** Two had soft engagement asks — *"Save this as your beginner-friendly guide"* and *"Follow IFM for Part 2"* — which are fine as far as they go, but neither gets us a name, a number, or a seat.

That's the gap. Good content, no ask.

---

## The four asks we use

### 1. DM keyword — our best one
Ask them to DM a single word. Then send them something real.

> *"DM us **GOLD** and we'll send you our one-page guide to buying gold the sensible way."*

Why it's the best: it starts a private conversation, it gets them into our DMs where we can actually talk, and Instagram treats DMs as a strong signal. **Every DM keyword must have something real waiting behind it** — a guide, a game link, a checklist. Never promise something we don't have.

### 2. Play the game
We have eleven games. **Not one of our thirteen tracked competitors has a single one.** This is our biggest advantage and we barely mention it.

> *"Want to see where your money actually goes? Play Money Map free — link in bio."*

### 3. Link in bio
For the course page, the masterclass, or a registration form.

> *"Doors for the September batch are open — 20 seats, link in bio."*

### 4. Comment prompt — use sparingly
> *"Which of these surprised you? Tell us below 👇"*

**Honest warning:** we've tried this twice and got zero comments both times. At our size a comment prompt only works if we reply fast and reply properly. Use it roughly once every five posts, not as a default.

## Worked examples — our own captions, before and after

**1. Gold explainer**
- *Before:* "…What do you wonder about gold? Drop your question below. 👇"
- **After:** "…Still not sure whether gold belongs in your savings? **DM us GOLD** and we'll send you our one-page guide."

**2. Independence Day post**
- *Before:* "…This Independence Day, don't just celebrate freedom. Start building yours. 🇮🇳"
- **After:** "…don't just celebrate freedom — start building yours. **Our next beginners' batch opens in September. Link in bio to hold a seat.**"

**3. Repo rate explainer**
- *Before:* "…Because finance gets easier when you understand what the buttons actually do."
- **After:** "…finance gets easier when you understand what the buttons actually do. **We teach all of it, jargon-free, in our beginners' course — link in bio.**"

**4. Asset allocation**
- *Before:* "…Save this as your beginner-friendly guide to understanding assets."
- **After:** "Save this one. And if you'd rather *play* your way to understanding assets than read about them — **DM us PLAY** and we'll send you a free game that takes four minutes."

**5. Workshop recap (our best-performing type)**
- *Before:* "…Here's to more learning, more confidence, and more financially empowered women. 💙"
- **After:** "…more confidence, and more financially empowered women. 💙 **Want to be in the next room? Comment SEAT or DM us — we'll tell you when the next one opens.**"

**6. Young Adults Workshop**
- *Before:* "…walked out with greater confidence, practical understanding and plenty of fun memories."
- **After:** "…walked out with confidence and a lot of fun memories. **We run these monthly. DM us WORKSHOP to know when the next one lands near you.**"

**7. Founder story**
- *Before:* "…confidence doesn't come from being an expert, it comes from finally taking charge of your future 🤍"
- **After:** "…it comes from finally taking charge of your future 🤍 **If that's the nudge you needed — our beginners' course starts in September. Link in bio.**"

Notice what changed in all seven: the caption itself is untouched. **We're adding one line, not rewriting anything.**

## Rotation — don't use the same ask every time

Across every five posts, roughly:

| Posts | Ask |
|---|---|
| 2 of 5 | DM keyword |
| 1 of 5 | Play a game |
| 1 of 5 | Link in bio / course |
| 1 of 5 | Comment prompt |

**Never a hard course pitch two posts running.** The rhythm is: teach, teach, invite.

---

## What not to do

- **Vague asks.** "Join IFM to secure your financial future" — join *how?* Compare with "DM us START". Specific beats inspiring.
- **More than one ask.** "Save this, share it, follow us and DM us" gets you none of the four.
- **Burying it.** The ask goes at the end of the caption, before the hashtags. Never mid-paragraph.
- **A keyword with nothing behind it.** If we say "DM us BUDGET", the budget guide must exist and go out the same day.
- **Corporate/workshop-for-companies pitches.** Different business, different funnel, not our job here.

## Before this starts working, two things need to exist

1. **The bio link — and this one is worse than it sounds.**

   The Instagram bio currently reads:
   > 💙 Financial Literacy Made Simple
   > 📈 Investing For Beginners
   > 🎓 DM for Workshops & Learning Programs
   > 👇 Build Confidence With Your Money one step at a time

   **There is no link.** The 👇 on the last line points down at nothing. Meanwhile `investingformummies.com/ifm` is live, with four batches, every session date, live seat counts and a working Pay button — and nothing on the profile sends anyone to it.

   **The fix takes one minute on a phone:** add the link, plus one line above it — *"👇 Sept & Oct batches — seats live now"*. Until that exists, a quarter of the rotation below is dead on arrival. *(IFM owns this, not the agency.)*
2. **The DM keyword replies.** For each keyword, a short saved reply plus the thing being sent. Start with three keywords only — **PLAY**, **START**, **WORKSHOP** — and add more once those work.

---

## The one-line test

Before any caption goes out, read it and ask:

> **"If someone loved this, what exactly do I want them to do in the next ten seconds — and does the caption tell them?"**

If the answer is "follow us, I suppose" — it isn't finished.
