## The short version

She can cover roughly **60% of the Growth & Distribution role**, and the 60% she covers is the part that needs doing first. Her existing strengths — Stories and phone video — map onto two of the highest-value gaps we have.

The missing 40% is the growth half. That's manageable if we're honest about it now, and fatal if we pretend it isn't missing.

---

## What she takes on — and why it fits

**1. Publish the backlog.** 38 finished assets sitting unpublished, against 46 lifetime posts. This is pure scheduled execution with no judgment calls required, which is exactly what a good EA does well. **Six weeks of unambiguous work that roughly doubles the account's output.**

**2. Every caption ends with a call to action.** Currently 0 of 12 captions have one. This is the single largest gap in the audit and it is a rule, not a skill: no caption goes out without a DM keyword, a link, or a direct question. She does not need marketing experience to follow it.

**3. Daily Stories, 3–5 a day.** Polls, questions, quizzes, sliders. She's already good at this — it's the reason this conversation is happening.

**4. Filming at every workshop.** This is her most valuable contribution and it isn't obvious, so it's worth spelling out:

> The workshop-photos post got **10 comments**. The founder-story post got **4**. The polished RBI and repo-rate explainers got **zero**. Real people in real rooms outperform produced explainer content on the only metric that indicates conversation.

She should be filming at every single session from now on — phone footage of real mums learning, reacting, playing the games. We have 188 raw session assets and they are our best-performing content category. She can generate more of it at zero cost, and she has the skill to cut it on her phone.

**5. Replies and enquiry logging.** Every comment and DM answered. At current volume that's about **one comment a day** — genuinely trivial, and it fits inside an EA role without strain. Log every course enquiry in a sheet. This is EA-shaped work and she'll be good at it.

**6. Capture contact details at every workshop.** Standing rule from the next session onward. Name, email, WhatsApp, from every attendee. Given we hold no list, this is one of the highest-value recurring tasks in the business.

## The trap that will kill this

**Outbound commenting will not happen, and it's the growth engine.**

Twenty to thirty genuine comments a day on other people's posts — adjacent creators, mum communities, Facebook mum groups — is what actually grows a 344-follower account. It is also:

- psychologically the hardest part of the job,
- invisible if skipped,
- and permanently outranked by EA work, because a diary clash is *urgent* and commenting on twenty posts never is.

**In a combined role, EA work always wins the time fight.** The predictable outcome: in three months, growth is still ~1.5 followers/day, and the conclusion drawn is "social didn't work" — when what actually happened is that the growth half never got done.

### Three ways to not lose this

1. **Ring-fence the time.** A named block in her calendar, defended like a meeting. Not "when you have capacity" — there is never capacity.
2. **Make it countable.** 20 comments a day, logged in a sheet with links. If it isn't counted it won't happen, and you won't know until the quarter is over.
3. **Or don't give it to her at all.** Keep outbound with Hiral, or accept slower growth and fill cohort #1 from the warm list instead. **This is a legitimate choice** — just make it deliberately rather than discovering it in November.

## Realistic time budget: 2 hours/day, not 4

| Task | Time |
|---|---|
| Publish from backlog + write CTA caption | 30 min |
| Stories (3–5) | 30 min |
| Replies, DMs, enquiry logging | 20 min |
| **Outbound comments (20/day)** | **40 min** |
| **Total** | **2 hrs/day** |

Plus workshop filming, which is event-based rather than daily.

**Do not pretend this is 30 minutes of work.** Allocate the hours explicitly rather than assuming they'll appear.

## Ramp — first 8 weeks

| Weeks | Focus |
|---|---|
| **1–2** | Read the Brand Guidelines doc. Play every IFM game — she can't write CTAs for games she hasn't played. Publishing from backlog on a fixed schedule. **Every caption and reply drafted for Hiral's approval before it goes out.** |
| **3–4** | Publishing and Stories unsupervised. Voice should be settled. Outbound commenting starts at 10/day. Filming at any workshop in this window. |
| **5–8** | Backlog substantially cleared. Outbound at 20/day and logged. Weekly Friday note running. Contact capture happening at every session. |

Two weeks of draft-then-approve is not micromanagement — it's how the voice gets transferred. Skip it and you'll spend three months correcting.

## Title and pay

If she's taking real scope, say so out loud. **"Executive Assistant & Content Coordinator"** or similar. A modest increase — meaningfully less than a separate hire, but real — plus a clear statement that if this goes well the social half grows and the EA half shrinks. Juniors who are told a role is expanding, and see it reflected in title and pay, stay. Ones who quietly absorb a second job leave.

## What this doesn't solve

She covers distribution and capture. She does **not** cover:

- **Course positioning, pricing, cohort dates, the funnel.** Still needs Role 2, the fractional strategist. That gap is unchanged — and per the audit, filling a 20-seat cohort from 344 followers isn't arithmetically possible without the warm list and the gated-game ladder.
- **Sustained outbound growth**, unless the time is genuinely ring-fenced and counted.

**Net effect on hiring:** Role 1 is deferred, not cancelled. Revisit a dedicated hire when either the social work is clearly capped by her available hours, or we pass ~1,500 followers. Role 2 is unaffected and remains the priority.
