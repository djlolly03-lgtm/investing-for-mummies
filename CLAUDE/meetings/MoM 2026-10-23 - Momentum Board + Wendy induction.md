### The gap nobody owns: the wait between paying and starting

Someone pays on the 20th and walks in on the 1st. **Nothing happens in between.**

- **Not a FOMO countdown.** "Four days to go" is wrong for this audience — twelve to fifteen women in a room, not a flash sale.
- **Something that adds value:** topical or educational pieces, an invitation to follow us on Instagram, a nudge to go through the games section so class lands better.
- The WhatsApp group can stay silent; **N8N can carry these individually.** First decide what we want to send and when — N8N only amplifies whatever we've decided.
- Noted as a caution: two messages a day, as some services do, is too much.

### The November 17-vs-16 date error — root cause and fix

Not a tooling failure. **Dates were agreed verbally, noted in a diary, and never confirmed back.** The fix is already in place: for December, Asba sent the dates in writing, asked for explicit confirmation, and only then entered them. **That confirmation step belongs in the SOP** — at ten batches nobody can rely on memory.

## 2. Decisions on how batches actually run

### Certificates
- **Eligibility becomes a prerequisite, not a given.** Attend a minimum — suggested **6 of 8 sessions** — *and* complete the feedback form.
- **Move them earlier in the final session.** Hiral reports nobody is waiting around for certificates at the end. Do them after the first hour, take twenty minutes, then carry on teaching and close the class normally.
- Certificates for people who never attended are sitting physically printed and signed. Photograph and record what exists.

### Makeup sessions
There is **no list of who has one, two or three classes pending.** Today we post next month's schedule to the group and ask people to tell us if they're coming.

**That doesn't work** — a group message about session 6 on the 23rd of next month is forgotten in two days. We now have paper attendance plus N8N: send the person an individual message near the date. *"Your repeat session for session 6 is on the 23rd, because you missed it last month."* Better communication here also makes an upsell natural.

### Game links after class
- Currently sent **only to Young Adults and Teens batches. Not IFM.** Extend it.
- **The old links are Vercel URLs and are wrong now** — the games are live on the site with their own links. Update them.
- Send by 5–6pm the same day, while it's fresh: people share with husbands and friends, which is free reach.
- Worth knowing: Stock Rush Pro had 3 views in 24 hours and one person actually hosted a game — we can see the activity but not who.

### Attendance
Paper is working. It is **not yet reaching the dashboard** — a photo posted in would be enough for now, and this data is about to matter.

## 4. ⚠️ DPDP — open, and it needs an expert

The card says we may photograph and record for marketing. **We are not taking consent, on the assumption that asking would get a no.**

**The problem raised in the room:** under the DPDP Act you **cannot rely on one group leader or manager to sign off on behalf of an entire group.** A corporate paying us does not make its employees' consent theirs to give.

- For regular batches, the feedback form carries explicit permission — that covers us today.
- **As numbers grow, DPDP consent needs to be part of the sign-up form itself.**
- **Action: Aditya to ask a contact running a DPDP startup for a consultant** who can advise properly. Nobody in the room knows enough to answer it, and that was said plainly.
