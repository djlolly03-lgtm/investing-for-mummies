The only question left is *"is your company interested?"* Several of these women sit inside substantial businesses — one example discussed owns Four Seasons and Omkar 1942.

**This list becomes Wendy's data.** Asba and Aditya keep building it in the meantime.

## 6. Sakshi's review moves — and the cadence changes

**Her 8-week review will not happen on Monday.** The new sequence:

1. **Ketaki has an informal offline conversation with Sakshi** — how has it actually been going, what's her experience
2. **The same conversation with Asba**
3. **Then all together, end of October or 1 November:** the review, a harder definition of Sakshi's role, and a four-month plan

**The four-month plan runs November through February**, and everyone's plan lands on the same horizon so the whole team is aligned to an **April plan**. Wendy's equivalent conversation happens by 1 December, on the same four-month frame.

**Why the role needs redefining rather than just reviewing:** one half of Sakshi's role — posting — is being done. The other half — commenting on other companies' posts in the same space — **has not been happening, and nobody followed up on it.** That is a shared failure, not hers alone, and the review should treat it that way.

**The principle behind all of this:** start running real review sessions now, while the team is small. Set the DNA early.

## 9. The LinkedIn bot

Demonstrated rather than decided, but worth recording because it changes what LinkedIn costs us.

- A **35-day content calendar** posting daily for both Hiral and IFM, alternating — one posts in the morning, the other in the evening
- **Drafts replies to comments automatically** and presents them for one-click approval
- **Tracks who follows and who views**, researches new followers, and proposes an approach. It suggested approaching a new follower for IFM; the better call was made to approach her as a potential *mentor* for Hiral instead
- **Sends a connection request first, the message only after it's accepted**
- **Rebuilt both LinkedIn pages** — banner, overview, featured posts, Instagram links
- Capped at **three messages a day** deliberately, to avoid the account being flagged

**Ketaki's suggestion, agreed in principle:** point it at companies and connect directly with HR contacts. Slowly.

**A technical note worth keeping:** LinkedIn, WhatsApp and Facebook cache a crawl of the website for months. **After any site change, re-inspect the URL on each platform** or they keep showing the old version.
