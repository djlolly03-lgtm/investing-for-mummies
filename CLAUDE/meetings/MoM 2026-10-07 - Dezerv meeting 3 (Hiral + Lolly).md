## What Dezerv said about itself
- 14,000+ clients, 5 cities, 920 pin codes, ~300 RMs, ~1,120 new accounts this quarter. 10,000 transactions a day, built to handle 2 lakh.
- Half the business is digital acquisition, half ultra-HNI.
- Every client met at least quarterly ("D90"), mostly monthly on Zoom with a pre-read. RM loses the quarter's pay if a review is missed. All calls and WhatsApp recorded as institutional memory, so an RM change does not reset the relationship. Unhappy client picks a new RM from five 20-minute calls.
- New product filed with SEBI: 40% listed, 60% unlisted, so private equity with liquidity from the listed half. Nobody else does this combination.

## How they build a portfolio
- Onboarding starts with Wealth Monitor, their app: pulls mutual funds, stocks, NPS from the depositories, banks via account aggregator with consent. Generates a live review in minutes against SEBI-prescribed benchmarks per category. They flagged that fund houses pick the flattering benchmark, so they re-map. AIF coming in six months.
- First question is "how much are you willing to lose", not target return. Everything goes in a written Investment Policy Statement. If a client wants to break their own equity cap in a euphoric market, they re-paper the IPS first.
- Asset allocation is 90–95% of the return; fund selection adds 1–2%. Rules-based deployment: 18 months near all-time highs, 8 months after a 7% correction, faster after 15%. Uncommitted cash sits in arbitrage/liquid. 20-year back-test on every portfolio for drawdown and recovery.
- Rebalancing: a switch triggers only if forward alpha exceeds 3% after exit load and tax; pauses if under 40 days to the one-year mark. Churn 12–20% a year, roughly one fund in seven. Top-ups and withdrawals hit the exiting fund first. Currently exiting Mirae large cap, which is Shivani's big holding.
- Gold at home and a second property are included or excluded per client, not by template. A primary residence is not counted.

## Fees, in their words
- One fee on every category except liquid and arbitrage, which are zero.
- **Fixed model:** 0.8% sliding to 0.5%; 0.35–0.45% for large; 0.25% floor at ~₹300 cr.
- **Hybrid model:** 0.25% fixed plus a profit share (now 6%) above an 8% compounding hurdle, with a high-water mark.
- Underlying fund TER: average 0.52%, cap 0.55%.
- Fee is taken from the portfolio's cash balance; deferred up to a year rather than selling units to pay it. (This answers the "does the fee deduction trigger tax" question: usually not.)
- Debt is yielding 10.5–11% **pre-tax**. At the top bracket that is ~40% tax including surcharge. REIT dividend portion (~6%) is tax-free, interest portion taxable. Arbitrage ~6.2% pre-tax, taxed at 15% LTCG. Exit loads on equity funds expected to disappear industry-wide from around January; one house already removed them.
