- **Fee: take the lifetime trail.** The debate is settled. The client pays the same whether they come direct or via IFM; Dezerv pays the referral out of its own margin. Say it without hesitating. Hiral can choose to cut her share in three years; there is no reason to give it away today.

- **Client size.** IFM will send everything from ₹2 to 20 lakh SIP-type clients up to Shivani-size tickets. Ask their ideal size so IFM filters at its end and does not waste their time.
- **Ask for the fee math as a document**, not a screen. Frame it as: "three more students asked us Dezerv versus Avendus, and we could not answer." Fixed vs hybrid vs a flat fee if they offer one, in rupees on ₹50 lakh, good year and flat year, pros and cons of each. Something IFM can repackage for students. The biggest trust problem is fee opaqueness in rupee terms, not the percentage.
