# Swipe Right on SIP — Card Deck v1 (for review)

Game: Gen Next-only MF game. Player has ₹10,000 (first salary), swipes through 10 cards drawn from this pool, can invest ₹2,000 in max 5. Five-year reveal per card, money outcomes only (no percentages), jargon gets tap-to-explain chips.

**Voice rules:** influencer pitches can be slangy/hyped; reveals use plain first-timer language, short sentences, "your ₹2,000 became ₹X".

**Like counts are inversely tied to quality** (traps 33K–120K, honest cards 31–203, judgment calls mid thousands).

**Draw rules per run:** 10 cards = 5–6 red + 3–4 green + 1 judgment call, shuffled.

**Scoring:** final corpus + Red Flag Radar (points for left-swiping traps). Benchmarks: "Hype Victim" (right-swiped all bait) vs "Boring Genius" (only green flags).

**Tap-to-explain chips needed:** mutual fund, NAV/unit price, NFO, index fund, expense ratio, direct plan, ELSS, SIP, small-cap/mid-cap/large-cap, flexi-cap, hybrid fund, liquid fund, exit load, lock-in.

## 🚩 RED FLAGS

### NFO bait (NAV myth)

**1. @WolfOfWadala · 84.2K likes**
"New fund launching Monday! Units at ₹10 ONLY. The old fund from the same company costs ₹847. Which one's the bargain? 🧮💰 #groundfloor"
→ Your ₹2,000 became ₹2,750. Not bad — but the "expensive" ₹847 fund would've made ₹3,750. A fund's unit price doesn't tell you if it's good. ₹10 just means it's new, not cheap.

**2. @MoneyBhaiOfficial · 52K likes**
"New fund = fresh energy! Old funds are tired, bro. This one's hungry 🔥"
→ Your ₹2,000 became ₹2,600. New isn't better — new just means there's no report card to check. Old funds show you 10 years of results before you give them a rupee.

**3. @LakhpatiLifestyle · 38K likes**
"I put my OWN money in this new fund (screenshot 📸). Window closes FRIDAY. Don't miss it!"
→ Your ₹2,000 became ₹2,800. The "closing Friday" thing? A sales trick. The fund reopened the next week — most funds stay open forever. Real investing has no deadline panic.

### Past-returns chasing

**4. @ReturnsRani · 91K likes**
"This small-company fund gave 82% LAST YEAR 🤯 Imagine 5 more years of this!!"
→ Your ₹2,000 dropped to ₹1,400 in year one, crawled back to ₹2,200. Last year's superstar is usually at its most expensive. You bought the party ticket after the party.

**5. @ChartChacha · 67K likes**
"5-star rating! Number 1 in its category! The graph only goes UP 📈"
→ Your ₹2,000 became ₹2,900. Okay, not great. Stars and rankings grade the *past*. The #1 fund from 5 years ago is almost never #1 today.

**6. @VibesVijay · 73K likes**
"I made 3x in crypto. Now I'm ALL IN on this hot momentum fund. Trust the vibes 🚀"
→ Your ₹2,000 became ₹1,900. Vibes aren't a plan. Funds that ride whatever's hot fall just as fast when it cools.

### Regular-plan pushers (hidden commission)

**7. @SIPnSlay · 61.7K likes**
"Defence stocks went UP 140% so there's a new Defence Fund 🪖 Use my link, I'll do all the paperwork for you, totally FREE babes 💅"
→ Your ₹2,000 became ₹2,100. Double trouble: you bought *after* the big rise, and "free" wasn't free — she earns a cut from your money every single year.

**8. @DostInvestments · 44K likes**
"DM me and I'll set up your fund account FREE. Zero charges, pinky promise 🤙"
→ Your ₹2,000 became ₹3,250. Decent fund! But he quietly earns from your money every year. The exact same fund bought *directly* (a "direct plan") would've given you ₹3,600.

### Guaranteed returns / scams

**9. @GuaranteedGuruji · 120K likes**
"24% returns EVERY MONTH. Guaranteed 🙏 My students never lose. Ever."
→ Your ₹2,000 became ₹0. The app vanished in month four. Real mutual funds are never allowed to guarantee returns — anyone who promises is selling a scam.

**10. @ForexFatima · 98K likes**
"Double your money in 6 months. Send to my UPI, I'll add you to the fund pool 💸"
→ Your ₹2,000 became ₹0. Real funds NEVER ask you to send money to a person's UPI. Your money should only ever go to the fund company itself.

**11. @SecretTipsBhau · 105K likes**
"Join my VIP group, only ₹999 🤫 Daily sure-shot fund picks. Screenshots in bio!"
→ Your ₹2,000 became ₹1,600 — after his fee and his "sure-shot" switches. If someone really had sure-shot picks, they wouldn't be selling them for ₹999.

### Thematic FOMO (buying after the rally)

**12. @TrendTaai · 58K likes**
"EV is the FUTURE 🔋 EV stocks tripled already and a new EV Fund just dropped. LAST CHANCE."
→ Your ₹2,000 became ₹2,050. EVs might be the future, but the prices had already tripled before you arrived. Buying the future *after* everyone agrees on it is expensive.

**13. @AIPaisaPro · 88K likes**
"AI will eat the world 🤖 This AI fund is your ticket. Don't be the friend who missed it."
→ Your ₹2,000 became ₹2,400. The theme was real — but a fund betting on one idea swings hard. It crashed 35% in year two before recovering. Could you have held on?

**14. @PharmaDidi · 41K likes**
"The pandemic proved it: pharma is the ONLY safe bet. Health is wealth, literally 💊"
→ Your ₹2,000 became ₹2,300. You bought right after pharma's big spike — then it went quiet for three years. One-industry funds run hot and cold.

### Churning / timing

**15. @SwitchKing · 49K likes**
"My rotation system 🏄 New winner fund every month. This month's pick is LIVE. Follow along!"
→ Your ₹2,000 became ₹2,400. Every switch cost you a fee and tax, and you kept arriving at each fund *after* its best month. Jumping between funds means paying tolls to go nowhere.

**16. @TimingTina · 55K likes**
"Buy this fund TODAY. I'll tell you exactly when to exit 😎 We only catch the ups."
→ Your ₹2,000 became ₹2,500. Tina called one exit right and missed two re-entries. The market's best days come without warning — miss a few and you miss most of the growth.

### Borrowing to invest

**17. @LoanLekeLakhpati · 33K likes**
"Genius hack 🤑 Personal loan at 14%, this fund does 20%. That's FREE 6% bro!"
→ The fund grew your ₹2,000 by ₹900. The loan cost ₹1,400 in interest. You paid ₹500 to lose money. Loan EMIs are guaranteed — fund returns never are.

→ It's a match 💚 Your ₹2,000 became ₹3,300 — after falling to ₹1,500 in year two. He warned you. Honesty about risk is itself a green flag.
