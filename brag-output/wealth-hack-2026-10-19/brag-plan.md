# Brag Plan: The Wealth Hack

## What is this app?
A scan-to-join live classroom game for young adults (Investing for Mummies): every phone guesses the monthly SIP needed for ₹10 Cr by 40, then the projector reveals that every age gets a very different number, and what five years of "soon" costs.

## The angle
The whole product is one argument: the number depends on your age, and "soon" is expensive. So the video is that argument, not a feature tour. Two jars (one started early, one started "soon") frame it; the middle is the real game doing its trick: a room guesses low, the projector reveals the real number, then waiting five years roughly doubles it. The lesson lands as the closing line, quoted from the game's own end screen.

Tone is polished and confident: few scenes, long holds, no jokes beyond the game's own tagline.

## Hook (first 2–3 seconds)
The two-jar image (sprout jar vs coin-tree jar) under a slow push-in. The game's own tagline lands one line at a time:
"One started early." → "One started “soon”." → "Only one of them is rich."

## Key moments (the middle)
- The big question on the projector: "To have ₹10 Cr by the time you turn 40, how much would you need to invest every month?" while five phones lock in one by one (Aanya 21, Zoya 22, Rohan 24, Meera 27, Kabir 29), counter ticking "N / 5 locked in".
- The reveal: "₹10 Cr by 40 · what each of you needs every month". Guesses (₹25k, ₹10k, ₹50k, ₹1.50 L) sit beside the real numbers (₹1.15 L, ₹1.32 L, ₹1.74 L, ₹2.69 L) that roll up, with coral "₹90.4k short" chips.
- Round 2, the cost of waiting: "Same target. But you do nothing for 5 years." Coral hatched "5 yrs lost" grows on each life-bar and the numbers roll up: ₹1.15 L → ₹2.31 L, ₹1.32 L → ₹2.69 L, ₹1.74 L → ₹3.68 L, ₹2.69 L → ₹6.25 L.

## Outro / punchline
Back to the jars. "The best month to start is this one." then the lockup: The Wealth Hack · Investing for Mummies, with the live URL games.investingformummies.com/the-wealth-hack.

## User flow worth showing
Host asks the question → phones lock in → "Reveal the real numbers" → the real numbers vs the guesses → "Now… what if?" → five years of waiting → "The best month to start is this one."
