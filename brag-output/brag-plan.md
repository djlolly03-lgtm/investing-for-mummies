# Brag plan — IFM Games homepage (games.investingformummies.com)

**What it is:** a games homepage where women learn money by playing one of forty short games from the Investing for Mummies course. No sign-up, no jargon.
**Who it's for:** mothers who nod along when money comes up, and their teenagers.
**What sets it apart:** you don't browse a catalogue. You tap the thought you came in with ("My savings just sit there. Is that bad?") and get the games that answer it. The best ones run live in class on a big screen.
**Funniest real claim:** "Not bad. Just unemployed." (the answer to idle savings), and "Some games need twelve women and a big screen."
**Visual hook:** a question every viewer has said out loud, set big in italic Lora.
**Real UI shown:** the question picker (chips → shelf of game tiles), plus real classroom screenshots (Swayamvar, The IFM Crorepati, Stock Rush).
**Tone:** `default`: warm, playful, clean. It uses the site's own cream/teal/navy/gold palette, Lora italic and Nunito.
**Share caption:** "Tap the thing you've actually said out loud about money. Get a four-minute game that answers it."
